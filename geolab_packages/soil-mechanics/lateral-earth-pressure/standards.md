# Standards Reference — Lateral Earth Pressure

## Rankine (1857) — On the Stability of Loose Earth
**Scope:** Active and passive earth pressure coefficients for cohesionless soil with horizontal backfill and smooth wall: Ka = tan²(45 - φ/2), Kp = tan²(45 + φ/2). Extended to cohesive soils with 2c√K terms.

## Coulomb (1776) — Essai sur les Problèmes de Statique
**Scope:** Wedge theory for active and passive pressures including wall friction (δ) and sloping backfill (β). Ka and Kp via trial wedge method. More general than Rankine but passive case is unconservative for high δ.

## AASHTO LRFD Bridge Design Specifications — Section 3.11
**Scope:** Lateral earth pressures for retaining walls, abutments, and buried structures. At-rest, active, passive coefficients. Surcharge loading, compaction-induced pressures, seismic earth pressure (Mononobe-Okabe method).

## FHWA-NHI-07-071 — Earth Retaining Structures
**Scope:** FHWA reference manual for design of retaining walls. Gravity, cantilever, MSE, anchored, and soil nail walls. Lateral earth pressure diagrams, apparent earth pressure for braced excavations (Terzaghi-Peck), global stability.

## NAVFAC DM-7.02 — Foundations and Earth Structures
**Scope:** US Navy design manual with comprehensive lateral earth pressure charts, including effects of stratified soils, surcharges, and seismic loading. Widely referenced in practice.

## Mononobe-Okabe (1926, 1929) — Seismic Earth Pressure
**Scope:** Pseudostatic extension of Coulomb theory for earthquake-induced lateral earth pressure. KAE and KPE coefficients incorporating horizontal and vertical seismic coefficients (kh, kv). Used in AASHTO for seismic retaining wall design.
