# Standards Reference — Rock Mass Characterization

## ISRM Suggested Methods (1978, 1981, 2007, 2015)
**Scope:** International Society for Rock Mechanics comprehensive suite of suggested methods for rock characterization. Key documents: Quantitative Description of Discontinuities in Rock Masses (1978) — orientation, spacing, persistence, roughness, aperture, infilling, seepage, number of sets, block size. Laboratory and field testing methods for intact rock and rock masses. The fundamental international reference for rock characterization methodology.

## ASTM D5878 — Standard Guides for Using Rock-Mass Classification Systems for Engineering Purposes
**Scope:** Guide to applying RMR, Q-system, and other classification systems. Procedures for data collection, rating determination, and application to engineering problems. Discusses limitations and appropriate use of each system.

## ASTM D5731 — Standard Test Method for Determination of the Point Load Strength Index of Rock
**Scope:** Procedure for diametral, axial, block, and irregular lump point load testing. Equipment requirements, specimen preparation, loading procedure, size correction to Is(50), and correlation to UCS. Portable equipment suitable for field use.

## ASTM D5873 — Standard Test Method for Determination of Rock Hardness by Rebound Hammer Method
**Scope:** Schmidt (rebound) hammer testing procedure for rock surfaces. Equipment calibration, surface preparation, number of tests (20 per location), data reduction (average of upper 50%), orientation correction, and UCS correlation.

## ASTM D6032 — Standard Test Method for Determining Rock Quality Designation (RQD)
**Scope:** Procedure for measuring RQD on rock core. Core diameter requirements (minimum NX), handling precautions, identification of natural vs. mechanical breaks, measurement procedure, and reporting.

## Bieniawski (1989) — Engineering Rock Mass Classifications
**Scope:** Definitive text on the Rock Mass Rating (RMR) system. Rating tables for all five parameters, orientation adjustments for different applications (tunnels, foundations, slopes), correlations to engineering properties (cohesion, friction, deformation modulus), and stand-up time for unsupported spans.

## Barton, Lien & Lunde (1974) — Engineering Classification of Rock Masses for the Design of Tunnel Support
**Scope:** Original Q-system publication. Parameter definitions, rating scales, support chart relating Q and span to support requirements. Updated by Barton (2002) with expanded database and Qc refinement.

## Hoek, Kaiser & Bawden (1995) — Support of Underground Excavations in Hard Rock
**Scope:** Comprehensive treatment of rock mass characterization for underground engineering. GSI concept, generalized Hoek-Brown criterion, estimation of rock mass properties from GSI, and support design. Updated by Hoek & Marinos (2000) for GSI refinement.

## BS 5930:2015 — Code of Practice for Ground Investigations
**Scope:** British Standard for ground investigation including rock mass description. Weathering classification (Grades I–VI), rock mass description methodology, core logging, and reporting. Widely used internationally alongside ISRM methods.

## Priest (1993) — Discontinuity Analysis for Rock Engineering
**Scope:** Statistical treatment of discontinuity data from scanline and window surveys. Spacing distributions (negative exponential), orientation analysis (Fisher distribution, stereonet clustering), persistence modeling, block theory fundamentals, and probabilistic methods for discontinuity characterization.
