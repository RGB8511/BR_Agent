# Standards Reference — Dam Hydrology

## USBR Design Standards No. 13, Chapter 2 — Hydrologic Considerations
**Scope:** USBR requirements for IDF determination. Defines hydrologic hazard classification, PMF development methodology, reservoir routing procedures, and freeboard criteria. Requires PMF for high-hazard dams unless a risk-informed approach (per USBR Public Protection Guidelines/PPL) demonstrates acceptable risk at a lower flood level.

## USACE ER 1110-8-2 (FR) — Inflow Design Floods for Dams and Reservoirs
**Scope:** USACE policy for selecting IDFs. Historically required PMF for all USACE dams with potential loss of life. Current policy allows risk-informed approaches consistent with USACE dam safety program. Defines procedures for PMF development, incremental damage assessment, and hydrologic hazard evaluation.

## FERC Engineering Guidelines, Chapter 2 — Selecting and Accommodating the Inflow Design Flood
**Scope:** FERC guidance for licensed hydropower dams. Covers hazard classification, IDF selection, PMF development, and adequacy evaluation. Requires PMF for most high-hazard dams with limited exceptions. Defines incremental damage assessment methodology.

## HMR 51 — Probable Maximum Precipitation Estimates, United States East of the 105th Meridian
**Scope:** Generalized PMP estimates for the eastern US. Provides depth-area-duration (DAD) values for durations 6–72 hours and areas 10–20,000 mi². Used with HMR 52 for watershed-specific application.

## HMR 52 — Application of Probable Maximum Precipitation Estimates, United States East of the 105th Meridian
**Scope:** Procedures for applying HMR 51 PMP values to specific watersheds. Includes temporal distribution, spatial orientation, and computation of basin-average PMP. Drainage areas up to 20,000 mi².

## WMO-1045 — Manual on Estimation of Probable Maximum Precipitation
**Scope:** World Meteorological Organization international guidance on PMP estimation. Covers moisture maximization, storm transposition, statistical approaches, and generalized methods. The authoritative international reference for PMP methodology.

## FEMA P-94 (formerly FEMA 94-0002) — Selecting and Accommodating Inflow Design Floods for Dams
**Scope:** FEMA guidance aligning with the Federal Guidelines for Dam Safety (FEMA 333). Provides framework for dam hazard classification, IDF selection, and adequacy evaluation applicable to all federal and state-regulated dams.

## HEC-HMS — Hydrologic Modeling System (USACE)
**Scope:** Standard software for PMF computation. Implements loss models, unit hydrographs, baseflow, snowmelt (temperature index), and level-pool reservoir routing. Used to develop the inflow hydrograph and route through the reservoir. Output: peak inflow, routed peak outflow, maximum pool elevation.

## HEC-RAS — River Analysis System (USACE)
**Scope:** Standard software for dam breach analysis and downstream flood routing. 1D and 2D unsteady flow modeling. Internal breach models (parametric and physically-based). Maps inundation extent, depth, velocity, and timing for EAP development and consequence assessment.
