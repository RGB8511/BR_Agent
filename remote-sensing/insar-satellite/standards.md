# Standards Reference — InSAR Satellite Deformation Monitoring

## ESA Sentinel-1 Toolbox (SNAP) & Documentation
**Scope:** European Space Agency's free open-source software for SAR processing. Includes co-registration, interferogram generation, DEM removal, filtering, phase unwrapping, and geocoding. Documentation provides the algorithmic basis for InSAR processing. Sentinel-1 data freely available through Copernicus Open Access Hub.

## CEOS SAR Calibration/Validation Guidelines
**Scope:** Committee on Earth Observation Satellites guidelines for SAR data quality. Radiometric calibration, geometric accuracy, phase stability requirements, and validation procedures for interferometric applications.

## Ferretti et al. (2007) — InSAR Principles: Guidelines for SAR Interferometry Processing and Interpretation (ESA TM-19)
**Scope:** ESA training manual and definitive reference for InSAR processing. Covers SAR fundamentals, interferogram generation, atmospheric effects, phase unwrapping, DEM generation, and deformation measurement. Standard reference for PS-InSAR methodology.

## Hanssen (2001) — Radar Interferometry: Data Interpretation and Error Analysis
**Scope:** Comprehensive textbook on radar interferometry. Statistical treatment of phase errors, atmospheric modeling, decorrelation sources, and accuracy assessment. The theoretical foundation for InSAR geodesy.

## ISSMGE TC220 — Field Measurements in Geomechanics (Remote Sensing)
**Scope:** International Society for Soil Mechanics and Geotechnical Engineering technical committee guidance on remote sensing for geotechnical applications. Includes InSAR guidelines for displacement monitoring, integration with ground-based instruments, and reporting standards.

## ICOLD Bulletin 158 — Dam Surveillance Guide (Remote Sensing Chapter)
**Scope:** International Commission on Large Dams guidance on remote sensing for dam surveillance. Satellite InSAR, ground-based InSAR (GB-InSAR), and integration with conventional geodetic monitoring. Recommendations for corner reflector deployment and data interpretation for dam safety.

## ISO 19157 — Geographic Information — Data Quality
**Scope:** International standard for describing and assessing quality of geographic/geospatial data. Applicable to InSAR-derived displacement products: completeness, positional accuracy, temporal accuracy, and thematic accuracy reporting.
