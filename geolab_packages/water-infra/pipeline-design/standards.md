# Standards Reference — Pipeline Design

## AWWA M11 — Steel Pipe: A Guide for Design and Installation (5th ed.)
**Scope:** Wall thickness design (Barlow), external load analysis, combined loading, fittings design, thrust restraint, lining (CML, epoxy), coating, cathodic protection, installation, and testing. The primary reference for steel water pipeline design.

## AWWA M41 — Ductile-Iron Pipe and Fittings (4th ed.)
**Scope:** DIP design and installation. Thickness design per AWWA C150, pressure classes, external load analysis, thrust restraint (mechanical, restrained joints, thrust blocks), polyethylene encasement, and installation procedures.

## AWWA M55 — PE Pipe — Design and Installation (2nd ed.)
**Scope:** HDPE pipe design for water systems. Pressure rating (DR method, HDB), external load (flexible pipe analysis), thermal effects, surge resistance, thrust restraint (fused joints), and installation. Covers butt fusion, electrofusion, and mechanical connections.

## AWWA M23 — PVC Pipe: Design and Installation (3rd ed.)
**Scope:** PVC water pipe per AWWA C900 and C905. Pressure classes, external load analysis (flexible pipe), bedding requirements, thrust restraint, and installation. Includes design for combined internal pressure and external load.

## AWWA C150 — Thickness Design of Ductile-Iron Pipe
**Scope:** Thickness design standard for DIP. Internal pressure design, earth load (Marston theory), truck load (AASHTO), and combined loading. Provides tables of net thickness by diameter, pressure class, and laying condition.

## AWWA C200 — Steel Water Pipe, 6 In. (150 mm) and Larger
**Scope:** Manufacturing standard for steel water pipe. Material properties, wall thickness, dimensions, welding requirements, hydrostatic testing, and tolerances.

## AWWA C900 — Polyvinyl Chloride (PVC) Pressure Pipe and Fabricated Fittings, 4 In. Through 60 In.
**Scope:** Manufacturing standard for PVC water main pipe. Pressure classes 100–305 psi. DR-based sizing. Material requirements, dimensions, testing, and marking.

## AWWA C906 — Polyethylene (PE) Pressure Pipe and Fittings, 4 In. Through 65 In., for Waterworks
**Scope:** HDPE pipe standard for water service. DR-based pressure rating. Material (PE4710), dimensions, testing, and marking. Covers butt fusion and electrofusion joining.

## ASCE MOP 79 — Steel Penstocks (2nd ed.)
**Scope:** Design of steel penstocks for hydropower and high-head water conveyance. Shell design (Barlow + external pressure + buckling), stiffener rings, expansion joints, anchor blocks, bends, wye branches, and connections. Combined stress analysis for temperature, pressure, and weight.
