# Standards Reference — Groundwater Monitoring

## ASTM D5092 — Design and Installation of Groundwater Monitoring Wells
**Scope:** Standard practice for the design and installation of monitoring wells in all geologic settings. Covers borehole drilling, casing and screen selection, filter pack design, annular seals, surface protection, well development, and documentation. The primary ASTM standard for monitoring well construction.

## ASTM D5521 — Development of Groundwater Monitoring Wells in Granular Aquifers
**Scope:** Methods for developing monitoring wells to remove drilling fluids and fine particles. Covers surging, bailing, pumping, and overpumping. Development completion criteria including turbidity, stabilization of indicator parameters.

## ASTM D4750 — Determining Subsurface Liquid Levels in a Borehole or Monitoring Well
**Scope:** Methods for measuring water levels: electric tapes, steel tapes, pressure transducers, acoustic methods, and airline methods. Procedures for measuring both water table and artesian conditions.

## ASTM D6724 — Installation of Direct Push Groundwater Monitoring Wells
**Scope:** Monitoring wells installed using direct push (Geoprobe) methods rather than conventional drilling. Pre-packed screens, sealed well points, and temporary casing methods. Applicable for shallow monitoring in unconsolidated materials.

## ASTM D6725 — Decommissioning of Groundwater Wells, Vadose Zone Monitoring Devices, Boreholes, and Other Devices for Environmental Activities
**Scope:** Procedures for proper abandonment of monitoring wells. Casing removal or perforation, grouting, surface restoration, and documentation. Critical for preventing cross-contamination.

## USGS Techniques of Water-Resources Investigations (TWRI)
**Scope:** Comprehensive USGS guidance on groundwater measurement and monitoring. Book 2 (Collection of Environmental Data), Book 3 (Applications of Hydraulics), Book 8 (Instrumentation). Includes detailed procedures for water level measurement, well testing, and data quality assurance.

## EPA Region Guidance Documents — Monitoring Well Design and Construction
**Scope:** Various EPA regional guidance documents specifying monitoring well requirements for RCRA, CERCLA, and Underground Injection Control programs. Requirements vary by region but generally reference ASTM D5092.

## State Well Construction Standards
**Scope:** Most states regulate monitoring well and water supply well construction through state water resources or environmental agencies. Requirements typically address minimum casing materials, annular seal requirements, well completion reports, driller licensing, and decommissioning. Always verify state-specific requirements before well installation.
