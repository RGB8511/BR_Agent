# Standards Reference — Canal Lining Design

## USBR — Design of Small Canal Structures (Aisenbrey et al., 1978)
**Scope:** Comprehensive USBR reference for canal design including lining. Concrete lining thickness, joint design, subgrade preparation, placement methods, and quality control. Also covers canal structures (checks, drops, turnouts) and their interface with lined canals.

## USBR — Canals and Related Structures (Design Standards No. 3)
**Scope:** USBR design standard for canal systems. Cross-section design, lining selection, freeboard, operational considerations, and maintenance access. Includes criteria for both lined and unlined canals.

## USBR — Lining for Irrigation Canals (1963, updated)
**Scope:** Historical but foundational USBR guide for canal lining. Covers concrete, asphalt, earth, and membrane linings. Seepage reduction, economic analysis, and construction methods.

## ASTM D4439 — Terminology for Geosynthetics
**Scope:** Standard terminology for geomembranes, geotextiles, geogrids, GCLs, and related materials. Foundation for all geosynthetic specifications.

## GRI GM13 — Test Properties, Testing Frequency and Recommended Warranty for HDPE Smooth and Textured Geomembranes
**Scope:** Geosynthetic Research Institute standard for HDPE geomembrane quality. Minimum property values, testing frequencies for manufacturing QA/QC, and recommended warranty provisions. The industry standard for HDPE geomembrane specification.

## ACI 350 — Code Requirements for Environmental Engineering Concrete Structures
**Scope:** ACI code for water-retaining and water-containing structures. Durability requirements, crack control, joint design, and watertightness criteria. Applicable to concrete canal linings where water-tightness is critical.

## USBR FIST 2-3 — Canals: Operation, Maintenance, and Repair
**Scope:** USBR facilities manual for canal operations. Inspection procedures, lining repair methods, joint maintenance, sediment management, and vegetation control. Essential for long-term lining performance.
