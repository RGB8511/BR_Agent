# Standards Reference — Outlet Works

## USBR Design Standards No. 14, Chapter 2 — Outlet Works
**Scope:** USBR design requirements for outlet works including intake structures, conduits, gates and valves, energy dissipation, and air vents. Covers hydraulic design, structural design, mechanical and electrical systems, and instrumentation. Defines drawdown requirements and operational criteria.

## FEMA 484 — Technical Manual: Conduits through Embankment Dams (2005)
**Scope:** Comprehensive guidance for design, construction, inspection, and renovation of conduits through embankment dams. Covers failure modes (internal erosion along conduits, structural failure), filter diaphragm design, compaction around conduits, joint design, inspection methods (CCTV, sonar), and renovation techniques (sliplining, CIPP). The definitive reference for conduit-through-embankment issues.

## USACE EM 1110-2-1602 — Hydraulic Design of Reservoir Outlet Works
**Scope:** USACE engineering manual for outlet works hydraulic design. Covers intake design, conduit hydraulics (pressure and free-surface flow), gate and valve hydraulics, air demand, cavitation, and energy dissipation. Includes design charts for gate loss coefficients and air vent sizing.

## USBR Design of Small Dams (1987) — Chapter 10
**Scope:** Outlet works chapter covering intake structures, conduits, terminal structures, and control devices. Design procedures and charts for small dam outlet works. Referenced extensively despite its "small dams" title.

## USACE HDC Series 320 — Gate and Valve Hydraulics
**Scope:** Hydraulic Design Criteria sheets for various gate types: slide gates, tainter gates, jet-flow gates. Provides discharge coefficients as a function of gate opening and head. Essential for detailed gate hydraulic analysis.

## AWWA M11 — Steel Pipe: A Guide for Design and Installation
**Scope:** Design manual for steel water pipe. Wall thickness design (Barlow formula), external load analysis, thrust restraint, lining and coating, cathodic protection, and installation. Applicable to steel outlet conduits and penstocks.

## USBR Engineering Monograph No. 42 — Cavitation in Chutes and Spillways (Falvey, 1990)
**Scope:** Also applicable to outlet works. Cavitation mechanics, cavitation index calculation, aeration for cavitation control, and surface tolerance specifications. Critical for high-head outlet conduit and gate design.
