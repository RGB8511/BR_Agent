# Standards Reference — In-Situ Testing

## ASTM D1586 — Standard Test Method for SPT and Split-Barrel Sampling of Soils

**Scope:** SPT procedure, equipment specs, and split-spoon sample collection.

**Key Requirements:**
- Hammer: 63.5 kg (140 lb), 760 mm (30 in) free fall
- Sampler: 51 mm OD, 35 mm ID (with liner) or 38 mm ID (without liner)
- Drive 450 mm; record blows per 150 mm interval. N = blows for last 300 mm.
- Clean borehole to test depth; minimize disturbance (no jetting ahead of sampler)
- Record hammer type, energy measurement (if available), borehole conditions
- Refusal: N > 50 for any 150 mm interval, or no advance in 10 blows

**Common Errors:** Inadequate borehole cleaning (loose material falls in), excessive water head difference causing piping, worn/damaged sampler, poor hammer energy delivery.

## ASTM D5778 — Standard Test Method for Electronic Friction Cone and Piezocone Penetration Testing of Soils (CPT/CPTu)

**Scope:** Continuous cone penetration testing with electronic measurement of q_c, f_s, and u₂.

**Key Requirements:**
- Cone: 60° apex, 35.7 mm diameter (10 cm²), 150 cm² friction sleeve
- Push rate: 20 ± 5 mm/s (constant)
- Pore pressure element: saturated with glycerin or silicone oil, u₂ position (behind cone)
- Zero readings: before and after each sounding
- Data recording: minimum every 20 mm (50 mm standard)
- Dissipation tests: stop pushing, record u₂ vs. time for consolidation coefficient estimation

**Calibration:** Cone must have known area ratio (a). Check q_c and f_s calibration in calibration chamber or against known standards.

## ASTM D4719 — Standard Test Methods for Prebored Pressuremeter Testing in Soils

**Scope:** Ménard-type pressuremeter test in pre-drilled borehole.

**Key Notes:**
- Borehole quality critical — oversize hole gives low E_M and p_L
- Probe length/diameter ≥ 6 for plane-strain assumption
- Pressure increments held for 60 seconds each; measure volume at 30 and 60 s
- Plot corrected pressure vs. volume; determine p₀, E_M, p_L
- E_M from reload loop slope (not initial loading — disturbed zone)

## ASTM D6635 — Standard Test Method for Performing the Flat Plate Dilatometer

**Scope:** DMT procedure, equipment, data reduction.

**Key Requirements:**
- Blade: 95 mm wide × 15 mm thick, 60 mm circular membrane
- Push at 20 mm/s (same as CPT — can be deployed through CPT rig)
- Readings every 200 mm depth
- A-reading: pressure at membrane lift-off (contact with soil)
- B-reading: pressure for 1.1 mm center deflection
- C-reading (optional): pressure at membrane deflection return to zero (closing pressure for u₀ estimate)
- Apply membrane stiffness and zero offset corrections
