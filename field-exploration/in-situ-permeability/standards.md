# Standards Reference — In-Situ Permeability Testing

## USBR 7310 — Constant Head Hydraulic Conductivity Tests in Single Drill Holes (Earth Manual, Part 2)
**Scope:** USBR procedure for packer (water pressure / Lugeon) tests in rock. Single and double packer configurations, pressure schedules, flow measurement, data reduction, and interpretation. The primary US standard for dam foundation permeability testing. Includes guidance on maximum test pressures and equipment specifications.

## ASTM D4630 — Standard Test Method for Determining Transmissivity and Storage Coefficient of Low-Permeability Rocks by In Situ Measurements Using the Constant Head Injection Test
**Scope:** Constant-head packer test method for low-permeability rock. Includes transient analysis for determining both transmissivity and storativity from injection tests.

## ASTM D4044 — Standard Test Method for (Field Procedure) for Instantaneous Change in Head (Slug Tests) for Determining Hydraulic Properties of Aquifers
**Scope:** Field procedures for slug tests — adding or removing a known volume (slug) from a well and measuring recovery. Covers both rising-head and falling-head configurations. References Hvorslev and Bouwer-Rice interpretation methods.

## ASTM D4050 — Standard Test Method for (Field Procedure) for Withdrawal and Injection Well Tests for Determining Hydraulic Properties of Aquifer Systems
**Scope:** Procedures for pump tests (constant-rate withdrawal tests) and injection tests. Test setup, pumping protocols, drawdown measurement at pumping and observation wells, and recovery analysis. References Theis, Cooper-Jacob, and Neuman interpretation methods.

## ASTM D5269 — Standard Test Method for Determining Transmissivity of Nonleaky Confined Aquifers by the Theis Recovery Method
**Scope:** Specific procedure for analyzing pump test recovery data using the Theis recovery method. T = 2.303Q/(4π×Δs') where Δs' = residual drawdown per log cycle of t/t'.

## Houlsby (1976) — Routine Interpretation of the Lugeon Water-Test
**Scope:** Landmark paper establishing the 5-step ascending-descending pressure test and systematic flow pattern interpretation (laminar, turbulent, dilation, washout, void filling). Standard-of-practice framework for packer test interpretation.

## Quiñones-Rozo (2010) — Lugeon Test Interpretation Revisited
**Scope:** Updated guidance on Lugeon test interpretation addressing common errors. Refined pattern classification, effective pressure calculation, and practical recommendations for dam foundation investigations.

## Freeze & Cherry (1979) — Groundwater
**Scope:** Classic textbook on hydrogeology. Comprehensive treatment of groundwater flow theory, aquifer testing, hydraulic conductivity measurement, and contaminant transport. Provides the theoretical foundation for all field permeability testing.
