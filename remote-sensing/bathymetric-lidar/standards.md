# Standards Reference — Bathymetric LiDAR

## USGS Lidar Base Specification v2.1 (2023)
**Scope:** USGS standard for lidar data acquisition for the 3D Elevation Program (3DEP). Includes requirements for bathymetric lidar: point density, accuracy, classification (LAS 1.4 bathy classes), metadata, and delivery format. The baseline specification for federal bathymetric lidar projects in the US.

## ASPRS Positional Accuracy Standards for Digital Geospatial Data (2023)
**Scope:** ASPRS standards for horizontal and vertical accuracy of geospatial data including lidar. Defines accuracy classes, reporting requirements, and testing procedures. Bathymetric data accuracy reported separately from topographic due to additional error sources.

## IHO S-44 — Standards for Hydrographic Surveys (6th Edition)
**Scope:** International Hydrographic Organization standards for bathymetric survey accuracy. Defines survey orders with total vertical and horizontal uncertainty requirements. Bathymetric lidar can meet IHO Order 1a/1b in optimal conditions. Used for nautical charting and navigation safety.

## LAS Specification 1.4 R15 (ASPRS)
**Scope:** Point cloud data format standard. Defines point record formats including bathymetric-specific classifications (Classes 40–45), waveform storage, and metadata. The standard exchange format for all lidar data including topo-bathy.

## JALBTCX (Joint Airborne Lidar Bathymetry Technical Center of Expertise) Standards
**Scope:** USACE center of expertise for topo-bathy lidar. Produces specifications for coastal and inland bathymetric lidar surveys supporting USACE civil works missions. Covers survey design, accuracy requirements, data processing, and quality assurance procedures.

## Guenther et al. (2000) — Airborne Lidar Bathymetry
**Scope:** Foundational technical reference for bathymetric lidar principles, system design, depth calculation, refraction correction, and error analysis. Published as a chapter in the CRC Manual of Geospatial Science and Technology. Widely cited for theoretical framework.
