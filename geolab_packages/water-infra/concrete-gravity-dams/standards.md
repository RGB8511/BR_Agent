# Standards Reference — Concrete Gravity Dam Design

## USBR Design Standards No. 14, Chapter 1 — New Concrete Dams
**Scope:** USBR requirements for design of new concrete gravity and arch dams. Stability criteria (sliding, overturning, stress), loading combinations, uplift assumptions, foundation treatment, thermal analysis, and instrumentation. Defines minimum factors of safety for usual, unusual, and extreme loading.

## USACE EM 1110-2-2200 — Gravity Dam Design
**Scope:** USACE engineering manual for concrete gravity dam design. Stability analysis (gravity method), loading conditions, sliding analysis (limit equilibrium), foundation investigation, uplift and drainage, and seismic analysis. Includes worked examples for typical loading combinations.

## FERC Engineering Guidelines, Chapter 3 — Gravity Dams
**Scope:** FERC guidance for evaluation and design of concrete gravity dams at licensed hydropower projects. Stability criteria, loading, uplift, foundation evaluation, and monitoring. Generally consistent with USBR/USACE but with some differences in required FS values.

## USACE EM 1110-2-2006 — Roller-Compacted Concrete
**Scope:** Design and construction of RCC dams and other structures. Mix design, placement procedures, lift joint treatment, quality control, thermal considerations, and structural design. Includes case histories and lessons learned.

## USACE EM 1110-2-6053 — Earthquake Design and Evaluation of Concrete Hydraulic Structures
**Scope:** Seismic analysis methods for concrete dams. Linear elastic and nonlinear dynamic analysis, response spectrum method, time-history analysis, Westergaard added mass, foundation-structure interaction. Defines OBE and MCE performance criteria.

## ACI 207.1R — Guide to Mass Concrete
**Scope:** ACI guidance on mass concrete for dams and other large structures. Temperature control measures (pre-cooling, post-cooling, low-heat mixes), thermal analysis methods, contraction joint design, and construction practices.

## USBR Concrete Manual (9th ed.)
**Scope:** USBR reference for concrete technology specific to dam construction. Mix proportioning for mass concrete, aggregate quality, durability requirements, testing procedures, and construction quality control. Covers both conventional and RCC concrete.

## ICOLD Bulletin 107 — Concrete Dams: Control and Treatment of Cracks
**Scope:** International guidance on crack evaluation and repair in concrete dams. Crack classification, investigation methods, causes (thermal, structural, AAR, foundation movement), and repair techniques (grouting, epoxy injection, cutting and sealing).
