# Standards Reference — Corrosion Fundamentals

## NACE SP0169 — Control of External Corrosion on Underground or Submerged Metallic Piping Systems
**Scope:** Comprehensive standard for cathodic protection of buried/submerged pipelines. Criteria: -850 mV (CSE) with CP applied, or 100 mV polarization shift. Covers design, installation, operation, and monitoring.

## ASTM G1 — Preparing, Cleaning, and Evaluating Corrosion Test Specimens
**Scope:** Procedures for preparing metal coupons, cleaning after exposure (chemical and electrolytic methods by alloy), and calculating corrosion rate from weight loss.

## ASTM G5 — Potentiodynamic Anodic Polarization Measurements
**Scope:** Standard method for measuring anodic polarization curves. Determines passive current density, breakdown potential (pitting potential), and passive range. Essential for material screening.

## ASTM G48 — Pitting and Crevice Corrosion Resistance of Stainless Steels
**Scope:** Immersion tests in FeCl₃ solution. Method A: pitting, Method B: crevice. Determines critical pitting temperature (CPT) and critical crevice temperature (CCT). Higher CPT/CCT = better resistance.

## ASTM G59 — Conducting Potentiodynamic Polarization Resistance Measurements
**Scope:** Linear polarization resistance (LPR) technique. Scan ±10–20 mV from E_corr, measure i. R_p = ΔE/Δi. Combined with Stern-Geary equation to get i_corr and corrosion rate. Fast, non-destructive field technique.

## ASTM G82 — Development and Use of a Galvanic Series for Predicting Galvanic Corrosion Performance
**Scope:** Guide for developing and using galvanic series data. Notes that published series are environment-specific — series in seawater differs from atmospheric or soil conditions. Area ratios must be considered.

## ASTM G102 — Calculation of Corrosion Rates from Electrochemical Measurements
**Scope:** Equations for converting electrochemical data (i_corr from polarization) to penetration rate (mm/yr or mpy) and mass loss rate (g/m²·day).

## ISO 8044 — Corrosion of Metals and Alloys — Basic Terms and Definitions
**Scope:** International standard terminology for corrosion. Defines general corrosion, localized corrosion, stress corrosion cracking, and all related terms. Harmonizes vocabulary across disciplines.
