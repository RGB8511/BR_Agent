# Standards Reference — Compaction

## ASTM D698 — Standard Test Methods for Laboratory Compaction Characteristics of Soil Using Standard Effort

**Scope:** Standard Proctor test. Establishes γ_d,max and w_opt at standard compactive effort (600 kN·m/m³).

**Methods:**
- Method A: 4-in mold, material passing No. 4 sieve (use when ≤ 20% retained on No. 4)
- Method B: 4-in mold, material passing 3/8-in sieve
- Method C: 6-in mold, material passing 3/4-in sieve (use when > 20% retained on No. 4)

**Key Notes:**
- Minimum 5 points to define curve (2 dry, 1 near optimum, 2 wet)
- Do not reuse soil — each point uses fresh material
- If oversize correction needed (material retained on 3/4-in sieve), apply rock correction factor per ASTM D4718

## ASTM D1557 — Standard Test Methods for Laboratory Compaction Characteristics of Soil Using Modified Effort

**Scope:** Modified Proctor test. Same methods A/B/C as D698 but at 4.5× the compactive effort (2,700 kN·m/m³).

**Key Differences from D698:** Heavier hammer (10 lb vs. 5.5 lb), greater drop (18 in vs. 12 in), 5 layers vs. 3 layers. Everything else (mold sizes, blow counts, methods A/B/C) identical.

## ASTM D1556 — Standard Test Method for Density and Unit Weight of Soil in Place by Sand-Cone Method

**Scope:** Field density measurement using calibrated sand to determine volume of excavated hole.

**Procedure:**
1. Level surface, set base plate
2. Excavate hole ~6 in deep, collect and weigh all material
3. Fill hole with calibrated Ottawa sand from cone apparatus
4. Calculate volume from mass of sand used minus cone correction
5. Determine moisture content of excavated material

**Key Notes:**
- Sand must be calibrated for bulk density in the cone apparatus
- Hole should not exceed mold volume from Proctor test
- Reference standard — all other field density methods calibrated against this
- Slow (~30 min per test) but most reliable

## ASTM D6938 — Standard Test Methods for In-Place Density and Water Content of Soil and Soil-Aggregate by Nuclear Methods (Shallow Depth)

**Scope:** Rapid field measurement of density and moisture content using nuclear gauge.

**Modes:**
- Direct transmission: Source rod inserted into hole (6–12 in depth). More accurate.
- Backscatter: Source on surface. Measures upper ~3–4 in only. Less accurate.

**Key Notes:**
- Requires NRC license and radiation safety training
- Must be calibrated per manufacturer specifications
- Verify against sand cone periodically (ASTM D6938 recommends comparison testing)
- Moisture measurement via hydrogen detection — affected by chemically bound water, organic matter
- Do not use near other nuclear gauges or concrete structures (radiation scatter)

## ASTM D5080 — Standard Test Method for Rapid Determination of Percent Compaction

**Scope:** Rapid one-point Proctor method using correlation with family of curves. Requires prior database of compaction curves for the soil type.

**Key Notes:**
- Not a standalone test — requires representative Proctor curves
- Useful for production QC when soil source is consistent
- Less accurate than full 5-point Proctor
