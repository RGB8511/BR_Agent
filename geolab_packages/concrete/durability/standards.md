# Standards Reference — Concrete Durability

## ASTM C1260 — Potential Alkali Reactivity of Aggregates (Mortar-Bar Method)
**Scope:** Accelerated test. Mortar bars stored in 1N NaOH at 80°C for 14 days. Measures expansion. < 0.10% = innocuous, > 0.20% = potentially deleterious. Fast (16 days) but aggressive — some aggregates with good field performance fail.

## ASTM C1293 — Determination of Length Change of Concrete Due to ASR (Concrete Prism Test)
**Scope:** Gold standard for ASR. Concrete prisms stored at 38°C, high humidity for 1 year (aggregates) or 2 years (SCM evaluation). < 0.04% at 1 year = acceptable. Slow but most reliable.

## ASTM C1567 — Potential ASR of Combinations of Cementitious Materials and Aggregate (Accelerated Mortar-Bar)
**Scope:** Modified C1260 that evaluates SCM combinations for ASR mitigation. Same procedure but with blended cement. < 0.10% at 14 days = combination is effective.

## ASTM C1202 — Electrical Indication of Concrete's Ability to Resist Chloride Ion Penetration (RCPT)
**Scope:** Rapid chloride permeability. 60V DC applied across 50mm concrete disk for 6 hours. Measures total charge (coulombs). Widely specified but measures conductivity, not actual chloride transport. SCMs with conductive pore solutions (slag) can give misleading results.

## ASTM C1556 — Determining the Apparent Chloride Diffusion Coefficient by Bulk Diffusion
**Scope:** Ponding test. NaCl solution on concrete surface for ≥ 35 days. Profile grind at multiple depths, measure Cl⁻ at each depth. Fit Fick's second law to determine D_a. More physically meaningful than RCPT.

## ASTM C1585 — Measurement of Rate of Absorption of Water by Hydraulic-Cement Concretes
**Scope:** Sorptivity test. Measures water uptake by capillary suction over time. Reports initial absorption rate (1 min to 6 hours) and secondary rate (1–7 days).

## ASTM C666 — Resistance of Concrete to Rapid Freezing and Thawing
**Scope:** Procedure A (freeze-thaw in water) and B (freeze-thaw in air, thaw in water). 300 cycles target. Measure relative dynamic modulus by resonant frequency (C215). Durability factor DF ≥ 60% is satisfactory.

## ASTM C672 — Scaling Resistance of Concrete Surfaces Exposed to Deicing Chemicals
**Scope:** Ponding with CaCl₂ solution, freeze-thaw cycling. Visual rating 0 (no scaling) to 5 (severe). Qualitative test. Being replaced by more quantitative methods in some jurisdictions.

## ASTM C856 — Petrographic Examination of Hardened Concrete
**Scope:** Microscopic examination of concrete thin sections and polished surfaces. Identifies ASR gel, ettringite, carbonation depth, air void characteristics, aggregate types, cracking patterns, paste quality. Essential diagnostic tool.

## ASTM C457 — Microscopical Determination of Air-Void Content and Parameters
**Scope:** Linear traverse or modified point count method on polished hardened concrete surfaces. Determines air content, specific surface, spacing factor (L̄), and void frequency. L̄ ≤ 0.20 mm required for freeze-thaw durability.

## ASTM C1012 — Length Change of Hydraulic-Cement Mortars Exposed to a Sulfate Solution
**Scope:** Mortar bars immersed in Na₂SO₄ solution. Measure expansion at 6, 12, 18 months. Evaluates sulfate resistance of cement/SCM combinations.
