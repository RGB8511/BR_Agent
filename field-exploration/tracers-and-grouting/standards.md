# Standards Reference — Tracer Tests & Grouting

## USACE EM 1110-2-3506 — Grouting Technology
**Scope:** Comprehensive USACE guidance on grouting for civil works. Covers rock grouting (curtain and blanket), soil grouting (permeation, compaction, jet), grout materials, equipment, quality control, monitoring, and verification. Includes GIN method, split-spacing, Lugeon testing, and real-time monitoring requirements.

## USBR DS-13 Chapter 16 — Cutoff Walls and Foundation Grouting
**Scope:** USBR design standard for seepage control through dam foundations. Grout curtain design, blanket grouting, slurry wall cutoffs, and deep soil mixing. Includes criteria for curtain depth, hole spacing, pressure limits, and closure criteria.

## Houlsby (1990) — Construction and Design of Cement Grouting
**Scope:** Definitive practitioner's reference for cement grouting in rock. Covers mix design, split-spacing procedures, Lugeon testing (including 5-step pressure tests), quality control, and specifications. Widely used for dam foundation grouting worldwide.

## Warner (2004) — Practical Handbook of Grouting: Soil, Rock, and Structures
**Scope:** Comprehensive reference covering all grouting types: cement grouting in rock, chemical grouting in soil, compaction grouting, jet grouting, and specialty applications. Includes practical guidance on equipment, mix design, injection procedures, and quality control.

## Lombardi & Deere (1993) — Grouting Design and Control Using the GIN Principle
**Scope:** Seminal paper introducing the Grouting Intensity Number (GIN) method. Defines the GIN envelope concept, stable grout mixes, real-time computer monitoring, and the principles of grouting control based on pressure × volume limits.

## Käss (1998) — Tracing Technique in Geohydrology
**Scope:** Comprehensive reference on groundwater tracing. Covers dye tracers (fluorescent dyes, salts, isotopes, particles), injection methods, sampling and detection, breakthrough curve analysis, and interpretation. International standard reference for tracer testing.

## Goldscheider et al. (2008) — Tracer Tests in Karst Hydrogeology and Speleology
**Scope:** ISRM/IAH guidance on tracer test design, execution, and interpretation in karst environments. Quantitative analysis of breakthrough curves, multi-tracer techniques, and regulatory considerations.

## FHWA — Compaction Grouting Manual
**Scope:** Federal Highway Administration guidance on compaction grouting for highway applications. Design, injection parameters, monitoring (heave limits), and quality control for settlement remediation and sinkhole treatment.
