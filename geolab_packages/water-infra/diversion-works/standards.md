# Standards Reference — Diversion Works

## USACE EM 1110-2-2607 — Planning and Design of Navigation Dams
**Scope:** While focused on navigation dams, contains detailed guidance on cofferdam design, river diversion planning, and construction sequencing applicable to all dam types. Cofferdam height selection, stability analysis, and flood frequency for diversion design.

## USBR — Design of Small Dams (3rd ed., 1987)
**Scope:** Chapter on construction diversion covers diversion flood selection, cofferdam types, diversion tunnel/conduit sizing, river closure methods, and first filling procedures. Foundational USBR reference for dam diversion planning.

## USACE EM 1110-2-2906 — Design of Pile Foundations
**Scope:** Relevant for sheet pile cofferdam design. Covers sheet pile wall analysis, bracing design, seepage control, and stability analysis for temporary cofferdams.

## USBR DS-14 Ch2 — Outlet Works
**Scope:** Relevant for diversion tunnel conversion to permanent outlet works. Hydraulic design of tunnels, gates, and energy dissipation. Many diversion tunnels are converted to outlet works after dam completion.

## FHWA — Technical Manual for Design and Construction of Road Tunnels
**Scope:** While focused on road tunnels, contains relevant tunnel engineering guidance including TBM and drill-and-blast excavation methods, rock support, concrete lining, and groundwater control applicable to diversion tunnel construction.

## ICOLD Bulletin 104 — Monitoring of Dams and Their Foundations
**Scope:** Relevant for first-filling monitoring after diversion tunnel closure. Instrumentation for seepage, piezometric levels, settlement, and deformation during initial reservoir impoundment.
