# Standards Reference — Drilling Methods

## ASTM D1452 — Practice for Soil Exploration and Sampling by Auger Borings
**Scope:** Procedures for advancing boreholes using auger methods including hand augers, continuous-flight augers (solid and hollow-stem), and bucket augers. Covers equipment, procedures, sampling through HSA, logging, and reporting.

## ASTM D6151 — Practice for Using Hollow-Stem Augers for Geotechnical Exploration and Soil Sampling
**Scope:** Specific to HSA drilling. Detailed procedures for advancing hollow-stem augers, maintaining borehole stability, sampling through the stem (SPT, thin-wall), managing heaving sands, and transitioning to other methods.

## ASTM D6286 — Practice for Selection of Drilling Methods for Environmental Site Characterization
**Scope:** Guide for selecting drilling methods based on project objectives, site conditions, contaminant types, and regulatory requirements. Covers HSA, rotary (mud, air, dual-wall), sonic, cable tool, and direct push. Applicable to geotechnical investigations as well.

## ASTM D6914 — Practice for Sonic Drilling for Site Characterization and the Installation of Subsurface Monitoring Devices
**Scope:** Procedures for sonic/vibratory drilling including equipment requirements, core recovery, sample handling, monitoring well installation, and documentation.

## ASTM D2113 — Practice for Rock Core Drilling and Sampling of Rock for Site Exploration
**Scope:** Rock coring procedures including equipment (diamond core barrels, wireline systems), drilling parameters, core handling, measurement of recovery and RQD, borehole deviation, and documentation. The primary standard for rock coring in geotechnical practice.

## AASHTO R22 — Standard Practice for Determining the Properties of a Soil Deposit
**Scope:** Overall framework for subsurface investigation for transportation projects. Includes boring spacing, depth requirements, sampling frequencies, and exploration methods. References specific ASTM methods for procedures.

## FHWA NHI-01-031 — Subsurface Investigations: Geotechnical Site Characterization (Mayne et al., 2002)
**Scope:** Comprehensive FHWA manual covering all aspects of geotechnical site investigation. Drilling methods, sampling, in-situ testing (SPT, CPT, PMT, DMT, VST), geophysics, laboratory testing, and data interpretation. The standard reference manual for US transportation geotechnical practice.
