# Standards Reference — Embankment Dam Design (Hydraulic & Seepage)

## USBR Design Standards No. 13 — Embankment Dams
**Scope:** Comprehensive USBR design standard. Key chapters for hydraulic/seepage design:
- **Ch 2:** Flood & Inflow Design Flood selection
- **Ch 5:** Protective Filters (filter design criteria, testing)
- **Ch 6:** Freeboard (wave runup, wind setup, settlement)
- **Ch 8:** Seepage (flow nets, numerical analysis, instrumentation)
- **Ch 16:** Cutoffs and Foundation Treatment (grout curtains, slurry walls, blankets)
The foundational reference for USBR embankment dam practice.

## USACE EM 1110-2-2300 — General Design and Construction Considerations for Earth and Rock-Fill Dams
**Scope:** USACE manual covering embankment dam design and construction. Zoning, materials, compaction, seepage control, slope protection, instrumentation, and construction quality control.

## USACE EM 1110-2-1901 — Seepage Analysis and Control for Dams
**Scope:** Detailed USACE guidance on seepage analysis (flow nets, finite elements), filter design, foundation treatment (cutoffs, relief wells, drainage blankets), and monitoring. The primary USACE reference for seepage design.

## FEMA 484 — Technical Manual: Conduits through Embankment Dams
**Scope:** Design and rehabilitation of conduits (outlet works, spillway pipes) through embankment dams. Covers filter diaphragms, anti-seep collars (largely discontinued), joint design, inspection, and renovation. Critical for preventing internal erosion along conduits — one of the most common failure modes.

## ICOLD Bulletin 164 — Internal Erosion of Existing Dams, Levees, and Dikes
**Scope:** Comprehensive ICOLD guidance on internal erosion assessment and remediation. Covers all four mechanisms (concentrated leak, backward erosion piping, suffusion, contact erosion), assessment methods, filter evaluation, and repair techniques. Essential companion to risk assessment.

## Fell et al. (2015) — Geotechnical Engineering of Dams (2nd ed.)
**Scope:** Comprehensive textbook covering all aspects of embankment dam design including seepage analysis, filter design, internal erosion assessment, slope stability, and deformation analysis. The standard graduate-level reference.
