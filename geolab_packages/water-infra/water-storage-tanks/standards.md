# Standards Reference — Water Storage Tanks

## AWWA D100 — Welded Carbon Steel Tanks for Water Storage
**Scope:** Design, fabrication, erection, and testing of welded steel water storage tanks (ground-level and elevated). Covers shell design (API 650 adapted for water), roof design, foundation requirements, seismic design (Appendix E), corrosion protection, and inspection. The primary standard for steel water tanks in North America.

## AWWA D110 — Wire- and Strand-Wound, Circular, Prestressed Concrete Water Tanks
**Scope:** Design and construction of prestressed concrete tanks. Covers wire-wound and strand-wound types. Tank wall design, base slab, roof, prestressing, shotcrete cover, seismic design, and leak testing. For large-volume ground storage (typically 2+ ML).

## AWWA D103 — Factory-Coated Bolted Carbon Steel Tanks for Water Storage
**Scope:** Bolted (factory-coated panel) steel tanks. Lighter-duty alternative to D100. Good for smaller volumes, temporary installations, or rapid construction. Panel design, bolting, gaskets, coatings, and erection.

## AWWA M5 — Water Utility Management
**Scope:** Includes storage sizing methodology for water utilities. Operational, fire, and emergency storage components. System master planning approach to storage needs assessment.

## AWWA M42 — Steel Water-Storage Tanks
**Scope:** Companion manual to D100. Practical guidance on steel tank design, maintenance, coating/lining, mixing/water quality, inspection, and rehabilitation. Comprehensive reference for tank owners and engineers.

## 10 States Standards (GLUMRB — Recommended Standards for Water Works)
**Scope:** Widely adopted minimum standards for public water system design. Storage requirements: minimum 1 day average demand; fire storage per ISO; minimum pressures 20–35 psi at service connections. Turnover, overflow, access, and security requirements.

## ASCE 7 — Minimum Design Loads and Associated Criteria for Buildings and Other Structures
**Scope:** Provides seismic design parameters (mapped spectral accelerations, site coefficients, risk categories) and wind loading criteria referenced by AWWA D100 and D110 for tank design. Chapter 15 covers nonbuilding structures including tanks.

## NFPA 22 — Standard for Water Tanks for Private Fire Protection
**Scope:** Fire protection water supply tanks. Capacity, elevation/pressure requirements, heating, circulation, and maintenance. Referenced when fire storage is integral to tank design.
