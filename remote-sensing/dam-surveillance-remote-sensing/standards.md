# Standards Reference — Dam & Levee Surveillance Using Remote Sensing

## ICOLD Bulletin 158 — Dam Surveillance Guide (2018)
**Scope:** International Commission on Large Dams comprehensive surveillance guidance. Chapter on remote sensing covers satellite InSAR, GB-InSAR, LiDAR, UAS, thermal imaging, and integration with traditional instrumentation. Provides framework for incorporating remote sensing into dam safety programs. The primary international reference for this topic.

## USBR Dam Safety Risk Management (Chapter 15 — Instrumentation and Monitoring)
**Scope:** US Bureau of Reclamation guidance on dam monitoring including remote sensing technologies. Covers performance indicators, threshold development, monitoring frequency, and data integration. USBR has been a leader in applying InSAR and thermal imaging to dam surveillance.

## FERC Engineering Guidelines — Chapter 14: Dam Safety Performance Monitoring Program
**Scope:** Federal Energy Regulatory Commission guidance for licensed dams. Monitoring requirements, performance indicators, and threshold-based surveillance. Remote sensing increasingly referenced as supplementary monitoring technology. FERC Chapter 9 covers monitoring of embankment dams.

## USACE ER 1110-2-1156 — Safety of Dams — Policy and Procedures
**Scope:** US Army Corps of Engineers dam safety regulation. Includes surveillance requirements, periodic inspections, and instrumentation monitoring. USACE is integrating remote sensing (particularly InSAR and UAS) into levee and dam surveillance programs through the USACE Risk Management Center.

## USACE ERDC Technical Reports on Remote Sensing for Levees
**Scope:** Engineer Research and Development Center publications on applying thermal imaging, electrical resistivity, and InSAR to levee monitoring. Includes field validation studies from Mississippi River, Sacramento, and New Orleans levee systems.

## FEMA P-1025 — Federal Guidelines for Dam Safety: Selecting and Accommodating Inflow Design Floods for Dams
**Scope:** While primarily focused on hydrology, FEMA dam safety guidelines reference remote sensing for post-flood inspection and damage assessment using UAS and satellite imagery.

## ISO 19901-8 / ICOLD Bulletin 118 — Surveillance Methods
**Scope:** ICOLD Bulletin 118 (automated monitoring) and related ISO standards cover integration of real-time monitoring data including remote sensing into dam safety management systems. Framework for data management, alarm systems, and decision support.
