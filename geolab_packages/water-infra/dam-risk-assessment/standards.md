# Standards Reference — Dam Risk Assessment

## FERC Engineering Guidelines, Chapter 1 — Dam Safety Performance Monitoring
**Scope:** FERC framework for risk-informed dam safety, including Potential Failure Mode Analysis (PFMA), semi-quantitative risk analysis (SQRA), and risk-informed decision making. Defines Category I/II/III failure mode classifications and requires periodic PFMA updates.

## USBR Dam Safety Risk Analysis Best Practices
**Scope:** Comprehensive USBR methodology for quantitative and semi-quantitative dam risk analysis. Covers event tree construction, probability estimation (expert elicitation), consequence estimation (RCEM), tolerable risk guidelines (f-N charts), and risk reduction evaluation. The most detailed U.S. guidance on dam risk analysis.

## USACE ER 1110-2-1156 — Safety of Dams — Policy and Procedures
**Scope:** USACE dam safety policy including the Dam Safety Action Classification (DSAC) system, tolerable risk guidelines, periodic assessments, emergency action planning, and risk communication. Defines DSAC I–V ratings and required response timelines.

## FEMA P-1025 — Federal Guidelines for Dam Safety Risk Management
**Scope:** Federal interagency guidance on risk management for dams. Covers risk assessment methodology, tolerable risk criteria, risk communication, and portfolio risk management. Applicable to all federal agencies that own or regulate dams.

## FEMA 93 — Federal Guidelines for Dam Safety
**Scope:** Foundational federal dam safety guidelines. Covers hazard classification, design standards, inspection, monitoring, and emergency action planning. Establishes the framework under which state dam safety programs operate.

## ICOLD Bulletin 130 — Risk Assessment in Dam Safety Management
**Scope:** International guidance on risk assessment for dams. Framework for risk identification, estimation, evaluation, and management. Provides comparison of national practices and recommends risk-informed approaches. Foundational ICOLD document on dam risk.

## Fell et al. (2015) — Geotechnical Engineering of Dams (2nd ed.)
**Scope:** While a textbook, this is the definitive reference on internal erosion assessment methodology. Covers all four internal erosion mechanisms, probability estimation, filter assessment, and event tree construction. Directly used by USBR and USACE in risk analysis.
