# Standards Reference — Geotechnical Instrumentation

## Dunnicliff (1993) — Geotechnical Instrumentation for Monitoring Field Performance
**Scope:** The definitive reference on geotechnical instrumentation. Covers all instrument types (piezometers, inclinometers, extensometers, load cells, pressure cells, settlement gauges), installation methods, data collection, processing, interpretation, and management. Includes instrumentation planning methodology. Required reading for any instrumentation program.

## USACE EM 1110-2-1908 — Instrumentation of Embankment Dams and Levees
**Scope:** USACE guidance on instrumentation for earthfill and rockfill dams and levees. Covers instrument selection, installation during and after construction, monitoring frequency, data analysis, and trigger levels. Includes seepage monitoring (weirs, flumes, collection systems), piezometers, inclinometers, settlement, and survey.

## USBR DS-13 Chapter 11 — Instrumentation
**Scope:** USBR design standard for dam instrumentation. Planning the instrumentation program, instrument types and selection, installation specifications, data acquisition, and performance evaluation. Companion to USBR dam safety program requirements.

## ASTM D6230 — Monitoring Ground Movement Using Probe-Type Inclinometers
**Scope:** Standard practice for installing and reading traversing inclinometers. Casing installation, probe operation, data reduction, accuracy assessment (checksums), and reporting. Covers both vertical and horizontal installations.

## ASTM D7299 — Vibrating Wire Piezometers in Fill and Natural Ground
**Scope:** Installation of VW piezometers in embankment fill and natural soil. Covers borehole installation, push-in installation, placement in fill during construction, cable protection, and initial readings. Complements manufacturer installation guides.

## FHWA NHI-15-005 — Geotechnical Instrumentation Reference Manual
**Scope:** FHWA training manual for geotechnical instrumentation. Covers principles, planning, instrument types, installation, and data management. Focused on highway-related applications: embankments, retaining walls, slopes, tunnels, and bridges.

## ISRM Suggested Methods — Rock Mechanics Monitoring
**Scope:** ISRM (International Society for Rock Mechanics) suggested methods for monitoring rock mass behavior. Covers extensometers, convergence measurements, in-situ stress measurement, and acoustic emission monitoring. Referenced for tunnels, caverns, and rock slopes.

## Various Manufacturer Technical References
**Scope:** Geokon, RST Instruments, Slope Indicator (now Dgsi/Durham Geo), Measurand (SAA), and other manufacturers provide detailed installation guides, calibration procedures, and application notes that supplement the standards listed above.
