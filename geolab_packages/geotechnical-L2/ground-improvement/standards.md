# Standards Reference — Ground Improvement

## FHWA-NHI-16-027 — Ground Modification Methods Reference Manual (2017)
**Scope:** Comprehensive FHWA reference for all ground improvement techniques. Two volumes covering: preloading/surcharge, PVDs, stone columns, vibro-compaction, dynamic compaction, grouting (compaction, permeation, jet), deep soil mixing, soil stabilization (lime, cement, fly ash), geosynthetic reinforcement, and lightweight fills. Includes design procedures, QC/QA guidance, and cost comparisons.

## FHWA-NHI-06-019/020 — Ground Improvement Methods (Volumes I & II)
**Scope:** Earlier FHWA reference manual (predecessor to NHI-16-027). Still widely referenced. Detailed design procedures for each method with worked examples.

## ASTM D6276 — Standard Test Method for Using pH to Estimate the Soil-Lime Proportion Requirement
**Scope:** Eades and Grim pH test for determining initial consumption of lime (ICL). Minimum lime content to achieve and maintain pH 12.4 for 1 hour.

## FHWA-HRT-13-046 — Federal Highway Administration Design Manual: Deep Mixing for Embankment and Foundation Support
**Scope:** Comprehensive deep soil mixing design manual. Mixing methods (wet/dry), pattern design, laboratory mix testing, strength variability, QC/QA, and case histories. Includes reliability-based design approach.

## ASTM D4546 — Standard Test Methods for One-Dimensional Swell or Collapse Potential of Soils
**Scope:** Measures swell potential of expansive soils. Relevant to lime/cement stabilization design — pre- and post-treatment swell testing to verify effectiveness.

## EN 14731 — Execution of Special Geotechnical Works — Ground Treatment by Deep Vibration
**Scope:** European standard for vibro-compaction and vibro-replacement (stone columns). Execution procedures, monitoring, and quality control requirements.

## FHWA-NHI-10-024 — Design and Construction of Mechanically Stabilized Earth Walls and Reinforced Soil Slopes
**Scope:** FHWA design guidance for MSE walls and reinforced slopes. Internal and external stability, pullout resistance, connection strength, facing design, and corrosion/durability of reinforcement.

## Holtz et al. (1991) — Prefabricated Vertical Drains: Design and Performance (FHWA-RD-86-168)
**Scope:** FHWA reference for PVD design. Drain selection, installation, spacing design using Hansbo's theory, smear and well resistance effects, instrumentation and monitoring.
