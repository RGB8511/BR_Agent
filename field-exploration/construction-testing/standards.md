# Standards Reference — Construction Support Testing

## ASTM D1196 — Standard Test Method for Nonrepetitive Static Plate Load Tests of Soils and Flexible Pavement Components
**Scope:** Static plate load test procedure for determining load-settlement relationship and modulus of subgrade reaction (k). Covers equipment (plates, loading frame, reaction, dial gauges), loading increments, settlement measurement, and calculation of k-value and bearing capacity.

## ASTM D6951 — Standard Test Method for Use of the Dynamic Cone Penetrometer in Shallow Pavement Applications
**Scope:** DCP test procedure, equipment specifications, data recording, and CBR correlation (log(CBR) = 2.465 - 1.12 × log(DCPI)). Includes guidance on interpretation and profiling through layered systems.

## ASTM D5874 — Standard Test Method for Determination of the Impact Value (IV) of a Soil (Clegg Impact Soil Tester)
**Scope:** Clegg impact hammer test procedure. Standard 4.5 kg hammer, 450 mm drop, 4th blow reading. Covers calibration, test surface preparation, and reporting. Also covers 10 kg and 20 kg hammers for different applications.

## ASTM E2583 — Standard Test Method for Measuring Deflections with a Light Weight Deflectometer (LWD)
**Scope:** LWD field procedure for measuring dynamic deformation modulus. Covers equipment specifications, plate sizes, drop heights, sensor requirements, surface preparation, and calculation of E_vd.

## ASTM D1883 — Standard Test Method for California Bearing Ratio (CBR) of Laboratory-Compacted Soils
**Scope:** Laboratory CBR test. Soil compacted in 152 mm mold at specified density/moisture; soaked 4 days (if required); piston penetrated at 1.27 mm/min. CBR = (measured load / standard load) × 100 at 2.5 mm and 5.0 mm penetration.

## ASTM D4429 — Standard Test Method for CBR (California Bearing Ratio) of Soils in Place
**Scope:** Field CBR test. In-situ test using portable loading frame. Piston penetrated into ground surface at standard rate. Rarely performed — DCP correlation has largely replaced it.

## AASHTO T 222 — Nonrepetitive Static Plate Load Test of Soils and Flexible Pavement Components for Use in Evaluation and Design of Airport and Highway Pavements
**Scope:** AASHTO version of plate load test. Nearly identical to ASTM D1196 but with additional guidance for airport pavement applications and k-value determination for rigid pavement design.

## FHWA — Intelligent Compaction Technology (IC)
**Scope:** FHWA guidance on using intelligent compaction roller-mounted measurement systems for continuous compaction monitoring. Covers CMV (Compaction Meter Value), MDP (Machine Drive Power), CCV (Compaction Control Value), and correlation with conventional point tests. Specification framework for 100% coverage QA.
