# Standards Reference — Surface Geophysics

## ASTM D5777 — Standard Guide for Using the Seismic Refraction Method for Subsurface Investigation
**Scope:** Field procedures, equipment, data acquisition, and interpretation for seismic refraction surveys. Covers geophone arrays, source types, time-distance analysis, and limitations (hidden layers, velocity inversions).

## ASTM D7400 — Standard Test Methods for Downhole Seismic Testing (Companion)
**Scope:** While primarily borehole, referenced here for Vs measurement calibration against MASW results.

## ASTM D6431 — Standard Guide for Using the Direct Current Resistivity Method for Subsurface Investigation
**Scope:** Field procedures for DC resistivity surveys including profiling and sounding. Covers electrode arrays (Wenner, Schlumberger, dipole-dipole), data acquisition, apparent resistivity calculation, and 1D/2D inversion interpretation.

## ASTM D6432 — Standard Guide for Using the Surface Ground Penetrating Radar Method for Subsurface Investigation
**Scope:** GPR field procedures, antenna selection, survey design, data acquisition parameters, and interpretation. Covers reflection profiling for utility detection, stratigraphy, and cavity detection.

## ASTM D6639 — Standard Guide for Using the Frequency Domain Electromagnetic Method for Subsurface Investigation
**Scope:** EM induction survey methods. Covers instrument orientation (vertical/horizontal dipole), calibration, survey design, apparent conductivity mapping, and interpretation.

## ASTM D6429 — Standard Guide for Selecting Surface Geophysical Methods
**Scope:** Decision framework for selecting appropriate geophysical methods based on target type, site conditions, and investigation objectives. Includes applicability tables for common geotechnical targets.

## USACE EM 1110-1-1802 — Geophysical Exploration for Engineering and Environmental Investigations
**Scope:** Comprehensive USACE manual covering all major surface and borehole geophysical methods. Theory, field procedures, interpretation, and case histories. Includes method selection guidelines for USACE projects.

## FHWA GEC-5 — Geotechnical Site Characterization (2017)
**Scope:** Chapter 9 covers geophysical methods for transportation projects. Practical guidance on method selection, procurement, quality assurance, and integration of geophysical data with borehole information.
