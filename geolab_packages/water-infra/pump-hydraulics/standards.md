# Standards Reference — Pump Hydraulics & Selection

## Hydraulic Institute Standards (HI)
**Scope:** Comprehensive standards covering pump nomenclature, definitions, testing, performance, and application. Key standards include:
- **HI 9.6.1:** Rotodynamic Pumps — Guideline for NPSH Margin
- **HI 9.6.3:** Rotodynamic Pumps — Guideline for Operating Regions
- **HI 9.8:** Rotodynamic Pumps for Pump Intake Design
- **HI 14.6:** Rotodynamic Pumps for Hydraulic Performance Acceptance Tests
The definitive source for pump engineering in North America.

## ANSI/ASME B73 — Specification for Horizontal End-Suction Centrifugal Pumps
**Scope:** Dimensional standard for horizontal end-suction pumps. Ensures interchangeability between manufacturers. Covers shaft, bearing, impeller, and seal dimensions.

## AWWA E101 — Deep Well Vertical Turbine Pumps
**Scope:** AWWA standard for vertical turbine pumps used in water wells and pump stations. Covers performance testing, materials, construction, and installation requirements.

## Sanks et al. (1998) — Pumping Station Design (3rd ed.)
**Scope:** Comprehensive reference for water and wastewater pump station design. Pump selection, piping design, electrical systems, controls, surge analysis, structural design, and wet well design. The standard reference for pump station engineers.

## NEMA MG 1 — Motors and Generators
**Scope:** Standards for electric motors. Motor efficiency classes (standard, high, premium), frame sizes, enclosures (TEFC, ODP, WP-II), and service factors. Relevant for motor selection for pump drives.

## DOE — Improving Pumping System Performance (Sourcebook)
**Scope:** US Department of Energy guidance on pump system optimization. Life-cycle cost analysis, VFD application, system assessment, and energy savings opportunities. Includes the Pumping System Assessment Tool (PSAT).

## ISO 9906 — Rotodynamic Pumps — Hydraulic Performance Acceptance Tests
**Scope:** International standard for pump performance testing. Defines test procedures, measurement accuracy, and acceptance criteria (Grade 1, 2, and 3). Complementary to HI 14.6.
