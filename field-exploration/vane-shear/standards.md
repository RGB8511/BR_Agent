# Standards Reference — Field Vane Shear Test

## ASTM D2573 — Standard Test Method for Field Vane Shear Test in Saturated Fine-Grained Soils
**Scope:** Definitive North American standard. Covers equipment (vane dimensions, rod, torque measurement), test procedure (insertion, rotation at 6°/min, peak and remolded measurements), calculations (Su from torque, H/D=2), and reporting requirements. Specifies both push-in and borehole-advanced vane methods.

## ASTM D4648 — Standard Test Method for Laboratory Miniature Vane Shear Test for Saturated Fine-Grained Clayey Soil
**Scope:** Laboratory mini-vane test on samples. Vane sizes 12.7–25.4 mm. Used for quick Su assessment on tube samples, block samples, or test pit soils. Also used for quality assessment of tube samples (compare lab vane Su to FVT Su at same depth).

## BS EN ISO 22476-9 — Geotechnical Investigation and Testing — Field Testing — Part 9: Field Vane Test
**Scope:** International/European standard for FVT. Largely consistent with ASTM D2573. Specifies rotation rate of 6–12°/min, vane dimensions (H/D = 2), and reporting. Includes guidance on time delay corrections.

## FHWA GEC-5 — Evaluation of Soil and Rock Properties (2002)
**Scope:** FHWA guidance for interpreting geotechnical field and lab tests. Chapter on FVT covers: Bjerrum correction, sensitivity interpretation, Su/σ'v0 profiling, comparison with CPT and lab results, and typical values for common soil types.

## Bjerrum (1972) — Embankments on Soft Ground
**Scope:** Landmark paper establishing the need for correction of FVT Su values for stability analysis. Back-analyzed embankment failures showed FVT Su overestimates operational strength, particularly in plastic clays. Developed μ correction factor vs. PI that remains the standard-of-practice.

## Chandler (1988) — The In-Situ Measurement of the Undrained Shear Strength of Clays Using the Field Vane
**Scope:** Comprehensive review of FVT theory, procedures, interpretation, and limitations. Discusses anisotropy effects, rate effects, progressive failure, and comparison with other Su measurements. Key reference for advanced interpretation.
