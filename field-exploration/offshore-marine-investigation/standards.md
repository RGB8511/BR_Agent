# Standards Reference — Offshore & Marine Investigation

## ISO 19901-8 — Marine Soil Investigations
**Scope:** International standard for marine geotechnical investigations for offshore structures. Covers planning, geophysical surveys, drilling, sampling, in-situ testing (CPT, vane, pressuremeter), and laboratory testing. Applies to fixed platforms, floating structures, pipelines, and cables. The primary international reference for offshore site investigation.

## OSIG (Offshore Site Investigation and Geotechnics) Committee Guidance
**Scope:** Society for Underwater Technology (SUT) OSIG guidance notes cover best practices for marine site investigation including geophysical surveys, geotechnical sampling and testing, and reporting. Guidance on platform selection, sample handling, and quality assurance for offshore investigations.

## IHO S-44 — Standards for Hydrographic Surveys (6th Edition, 2020)
**Scope:** International Hydrographic Organization standards for bathymetric survey accuracy. Defines survey orders (Exclusive through Order 2) with total horizontal and vertical uncertainty requirements. Basis for specifying bathymetric survey quality for engineering projects, navigation, and dredging.

## DNV-RP-C212 — Offshore Soil Mechanics and Geotechnical Engineering
**Scope:** Det Norske Veritas recommended practice for geotechnical design of offshore structures. Covers site investigation requirements, soil parameter characterization, shallow and deep foundation design, and pipeline geotechnics. References ISO 19901-8 for investigation standards.

## ASTM D4823 — Standard Guide for Core Sampling Submerged, Unconsolidated Sediments
**Scope:** Guidance on gravity, piston, and vibratory coring in submerged sediments. Core barrel selection, deployment procedures, core handling, and sample preservation. Applicable to rivers, lakes, harbors, and offshore environments.

## ASTM D6032 — Standard Test Method for Determining Rock Quality Designation (RQD) of Rock Core
**Scope:** While primarily onshore, applicable to offshore rock coring. RQD determination from recovered core. Referenced in marine investigation when rock coring is performed from jack-up or vessel-based drilling.

## IMCA S 018 — Guidelines for the Design and Operation of Dynamically Positioned Vessels (Rev. 1)
**Scope:** International Marine Contractors Association guidance on DP vessel operations. Relevant for geotechnical investigation from DP vessels: station-keeping requirements, reference systems, weather limitations, and emergency procedures during drilling/CPT operations.

## CIRIA C683 — The Rock Manual (2nd Edition)
**Scope:** While primarily a construction manual, Chapter 4 covers marine site investigation for coastal/marine structures including bathymetric surveying, geophysical methods, sampling, and in-situ testing in marine environments. Widely referenced in Europe for marine ground investigation.
