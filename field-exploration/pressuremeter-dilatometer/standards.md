# Standards Reference — Pressuremeter & Flat Dilatometer

## ASTM D4719 — Standard Test Methods for Prebored Pressuremeter Testing in Soils
**Scope:** Procedures for prebored (Menard-type) pressuremeter testing. Borehole preparation, probe specifications, test procedures (monotonic loading with unload-reload loops), data reduction (p₀, pf, pL, EM), calibration, and reporting. Covers both gas-pressure and water-pressure systems.

## ASTM D6635 — Standard Test Method for Performing the Flat Plate Dilatometer
**Scope:** Procedures for DMT. Blade specifications (95 mm wide, 14 mm thick, 60 mm membrane), push procedures, A and B reading protocol, membrane calibration (ΔA, ΔB corrections), intermediate parameter calculation (ID, KD, ED), and reporting. Specifies testing at 200 mm depth intervals.

## BS EN ISO 22476-4 — Menard Pressuremeter Test
**Scope:** European/international standard for Menard PMT. Consistent with ASTM D4719 but with additional probe categories (AX, BX, NX) and more detailed borehole quality requirements.

## BS EN ISO 22476-5 — Flexible Dilatometer Test
**Scope:** European standard for dilatometer tests in rock (Goodman Jack type) and stiff soils. Differs from DMT — this is a borehole-based dilatometer with radial expansion, not the Marchetti flat blade.

## BS EN ISO 22476-11 — Flat Dilatometer Test
**Scope:** European/international standard for the Marchetti DMT. Blade specifications, testing procedure, calibration, data reduction, and reporting. Consistent with ASTM D6635.

## NF P 94-261 — French Standard for Foundation Design Using Pressuremeter Results
**Scope:** The French standard for direct foundation design from Menard PMT results. Bearing capacity from pL* (net limit pressure) using empirical factors for foundation shape, depth, and soil type. Settlement from EM using Menard's elastic method with rheological factor α. The most comprehensive direct-design framework for PMT results.

## Marchetti (1980) — In Situ Tests by Flat Dilatometer (ASCE JGED)
**Scope:** Original paper defining the DMT test, intermediate parameters (ID, KD, ED), and correlations to engineering properties (soil type, K₀, OCR, Su, M, φ', consolidation). The foundational reference for all DMT interpretation.

## Marchetti et al. (2001) — The Flat Dilatometer Test (DMT) in Soil Investigations (ISSMGE TC16 Report)
**Scope:** Comprehensive update of DMT interpretation practice. Revised correlations, additional applications (liquefaction, compaction control, ground improvement verification), worked examples, and comparison with other tests.
