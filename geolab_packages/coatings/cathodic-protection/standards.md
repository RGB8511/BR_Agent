# Standards Reference — Cathodic Protection

## NACE SP0169 — Control of External Corrosion on Underground or Submerged Metallic Piping Systems
**Scope:** Primary CP standard for buried pipelines. Protection criteria (-850 mV, 100 mV shift), design considerations, monitoring (test stations, CIPS), maintenance, and records. Referenced by most pipeline regulations (49 CFR 192/195 in US).

## NACE SP0176 — Corrosion Control of Submerged Areas of Permanently Installed Steel Offshore Structures
**Scope:** CP design for offshore platforms. Galvanic anode design, current density requirements by water depth and temperature, monitoring, retrofit CP.

## NACE SP0290 — Cathodic Protection of Reinforcing Steel in Atmospherically Exposed Concrete Structures
**Scope:** CP for concrete structures — bridge decks, parking garages, marine piles. Impressed current and galvanic (zinc) systems. Anode types: conductive coatings, mesh anodes, embedded discrete anodes, thermal-sprayed zinc.

## NACE TM0497 — Measurement Techniques Related to Criteria for Cathodic Protection on Underground or Submerged Metallic Piping Systems
**Scope:** Field measurement procedures for pipe-to-soil potentials, reference electrode placement, IR drop considerations, current interruption, and CIPS techniques.

## DNV-RP-B401 — Cathodic Protection Design
**Scope:** Internationally recognized recommended practice for CP design of marine and offshore structures. Current density requirements by temperature/depth zone, anode material properties, coating breakdown factors, anode resistance formulas, and design life methodology.

## ASTM C876 — Standard Test Method for Corrosion Potentials of Uncoated Reinforcing Steel in Concrete
**Scope:** Half-cell potential survey on concrete surfaces using CSE. Maps corrosion probability: < -350 mV CSE = >90% probability of active corrosion; > -200 mV = >90% probability no corrosion.

## EN 12954 — Cathodic Protection of Buried or Immersed Metallic Structures — General Principles
**Scope:** European standard for CP design and operation. Protection criteria compatible with NACE SP0169. Addresses DC stray current interference and mitigation.

## 49 CFR Parts 192 & 195 (US Federal Regulations)
**Scope:** US Department of Transportation regulations for gas (192) and hazardous liquid (195) pipelines. Mandate CP for new pipelines, monitoring at least annually, and protection criteria per NACE SP0169.
