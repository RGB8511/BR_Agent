# Standards Reference — Rock Mass Classification (RMR & Q-System)

## Bieniawski (1989) — Engineering Rock Mass Classifications
**Scope:** RMR system. Six parameters: UCS, RQD, joint spacing, joint conditions, groundwater, orientation adjustment. Rating 0–100, five classes (I–V). Support recommendations for 10m span tunnels.

## Barton, Lien & Lunde (1974) — Engineering Classification of Rock Masses for Design of Tunnel Support
**Scope:** Q-system. Six parameters in three quotients: (RQD/Jn)(Jr/Ja)(Jw/SRF). Range 0.001–1000. Support design via Q-De chart (Equivalent Dimension = span/ESR).

## Hoek, Kaiser & Bawden (1995) — Support of Underground Excavations in Hard Rock
**Scope:** GSI (Geological Strength Index) as simplified evolution of RMR for Hoek-Brown criterion. Visual assessment of rock structure and surface conditions. GSI charts for blocky, disturbed, and foliated/sheared rock masses.

## ISRM Suggested Methods — Rock Characterization, Testing and Monitoring (Brown, 1981)
**Scope:** International standards for rock mass description, discontinuity surveys, drill core logging, and index testing. Basis for RQD measurement and discontinuity characterization.

## ASTM D6032 — Standard Test Method for Determining Rock Quality Designation (RQD)
**Scope:** Procedures for determining RQD from drill core. Count lengths of intact core ≥ 100 mm (4 in) in a run. RQD = (Σ pieces ≥ 100mm / total run length) × 100%.

## FHWA-IF-99-025 — Rock Slopes Reference Manual
**Scope:** FHWA guidance on rock slope design, classification, and support. Includes RMR and Q-system applications to highway rock cuts.
