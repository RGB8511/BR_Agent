# Standards Reference — Embankment Dams

## USBR Design Standards No. 13 — Embankment Dams (DS-13)

**Scope:** Comprehensive design standard for USBR embankment dams. 21 chapters covering all aspects.

**Key Chapters:**
- Ch. 1: General design standards
- Ch. 2: Embankment design
- Ch. 3: Construction (compaction specs, placement, QC)
- Ch. 4: Static stability analyses (FS criteria, analysis methods, loading conditions)
- Ch. 5: Protective filters (NEF criteria, design procedures, chimney/blanket/toe drains)
- Ch. 6: Freeboard (wave analysis, wind setup, settlement allowance)
- Ch. 7: Riprap design (sizing, gradation, layer thickness, bedding)
- Ch. 8: Seepage (analysis methods, seepage control, relief wells)
- Ch. 9: Static deformation analysis
- Ch. 10: Embankment construction diversion and care of water
- Ch. 11: Instrumentation (selection, installation, monitoring plans)
- Ch. 13: Seismic analysis and design

## USACE EM 1110-2-1901 — Seepage Analysis and Control for Dams

**Scope:** Seepage analysis methods and control measures for USACE dams.

**Key Content:**
- Flow net construction (hand and computer methods)
- Underseepage analysis using blanket theory
- Design of relief wells
- Seepage control measures (cutoff walls, blankets, drains, grouting)
- Instrumentation for seepage monitoring

## USACE EM 1110-2-1902 — Slope Stability

**Scope:** Slope stability analysis procedures for USACE embankment dams.

**Key Content:**
- Loading conditions (end of construction, steady seepage, rapid drawdown, seismic)
- Required FS for each condition
- Shear strength selection (UU, CU, CD, SHANSEP)
- Method selection (Bishop, Spencer, Morgenstern-Price)
- Rapid drawdown three-stage analysis

## USACE EM 1110-2-1911 — Construction Control for Earth and Rock-Fill Dams

**Scope:** QC procedures during dam construction.

**Key Content:** Compaction testing frequency, moisture control, material processing, placement procedures, documentation.

## FEMA 484 — Technical Manual: Conduits through Embankment Dams

**Scope:** Design, inspection, and repair of conduits (outlet works, spillway pipes) passing through embankment dams.

**Key Concerns:** Conduit-embankment interface is a preferential seepage path. Requires anti-seep filters, careful compaction, and monitoring. Deteriorating conduits can lead to internal erosion.

## ICOLD Bulletin 164 — Internal Erosion of Existing Dams, Levees and Dikes

**Scope:** International guidance on assessment and management of internal erosion risk.

**Key Content:** Mechanisms, detection, event tree analysis for risk assessment, remediation options.
