# Standards Reference — Pipeline Structural Design

## ASCE Standard 15 — Standard Practice for Direct Design of Buried Precast Concrete Pipe Using Standard Installations (SIDD)
**Scope:** Modern direct design method for rigid concrete pipe. Four standard installation types with defined bedding, haunch, and compaction requirements. Replaces traditional Marston-Spangler indirect design for concrete pipe. Provides earth load, live load, and bedding factor calculations for each installation type.

## AWWA C150 — Thickness Design of Ductile-Iron Pipe
**Scope:** Structural design of DIP for combined internal pressure and external loading. Includes Marston-Spangler earth loads, AASHTO truck loads, and trench width considerations. Provides thickness tables by diameter, pressure class, and laying condition.

## AWWA M11 — Steel Pipe: A Guide for Design and Installation
**Scope:** Chapter on external loads covers Marston theory for trench and embankment conditions, Iowa formula for deflection, buckling analysis, and live loads. Provides E's values and design methodology for buried steel pipe.

## ASTM C76 — Standard Specification for Reinforced Concrete Culvert, Storm Drain, and Sewer Pipe
**Scope:** Manufacturing standard for reinforced concrete pipe (RCP). Five strength classes (I–V) with D-load ratings for 0.3mm crack and ultimate strength. Pipe sizes 300–3600 mm.

## AASHTO LRFD Bridge Design Specifications — Section 12: Buried Structures
**Scope:** Load and resistance factor design for buried structures including pipe. Earth pressure, live load distribution, load factors, and resistance factors for various pipe materials. The current standard for highway-loaded buried pipe.

## Moser & Folkman (2008) — Buried Pipe Design (3rd ed.)
**Scope:** Comprehensive textbook covering Marston loads, Iowa formula, rigid pipe design, flexible pipe design, and SIDD methodology. Includes worked examples for all common pipe materials. The standard reference for buried pipe structural design.

## ASTM D2412 — Determination of External Loading Characteristics of Plastic Pipe by Parallel-Plate Loading
**Scope:** Test method for pipe stiffness of flexible pipe. Measures force vs. deflection at 5% diametral deflection. Results used to calculate pipe stiffness (PS) and stiffness factor (EI/D³) for Iowa formula design.
