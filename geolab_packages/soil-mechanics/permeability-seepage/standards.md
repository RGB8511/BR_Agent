# Standards Reference — Permeability & Seepage

## ASTM D2434 — Standard Test Method for Permeability of Granular Soils (Constant Head)

**Scope:** Laboratory determination of k for granular soils using constant-head permeameter.

**Key Requirements:**
- Specimen: reconstituted or compacted granular soil
- Apply constant head difference across specimen; measure flow rate at steady state
- Minimum 3 trials at different gradients; k should be independent of gradient (confirms laminar flow)
- Correct to 20°C standard temperature
- Maximum particle size: 3/4 in (19 mm) for 6-in diameter mold

## ASTM D5084 — Standard Test Methods for Measurement of Hydraulic Conductivity of Saturated Porous Materials Using a Flexible Wall Permeameter

**Scope:** Laboratory k measurement for all soil types, especially fine-grained, using triaxial-type cell with backpressure saturation.

**Methods:** Constant head, falling head, constant flow rate, or constant volume.

**Key Advantages over Rigid-Wall:**
- Backpressure saturation ensures S ≈ 100% (critical for accurate k measurement)
- Effective confining stress can be controlled
- No sidewall leakage (membrane seals against specimen)
- Preferred for fine-grained soils and all soils where saturation is important

**Key Requirements:**
- B-value ≥ 0.95 before starting permeation
- Hydraulic gradient: typically i = 5–30 for low-k soils; must not exceed effective stress
- Steady-state criteria: 4+ consecutive readings with ≤ 25% variation
- Report effective confining stress, void ratio, saturation

## ASTM D4511 — Standard Test Method for Hydraulic Conductivity of Essentially Saturated Peat

**Scope:** Constant-head permeability for peat and highly organic soils, which compress significantly under testing loads.

## ASTM D5856 — Standard Test Method for Measurement of Hydraulic Conductivity of Porous Material Using a Rigid-Wall, Compaction-Mold Permeameter

**Scope:** k measurement on compacted specimens in compaction molds (rigid wall). Used for compaction QC of clay liners and embankment cores.

**Key Notes:**
- Simpler setup than D5084 but sidewall leakage is a concern
- Cannot apply backpressure — saturation may be incomplete
- Appropriate for k > ~10⁻⁷ cm/s; use D5084 for lower k values
