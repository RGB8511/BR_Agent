# Standards Reference — Seismic & Liquefaction

## ASCE 7 — Minimum Design Loads for Buildings and Other Structures
**Scope:** Chapter 11–23 cover seismic design. Site class definitions (A–F) based on Vs30, N̄, or s̄u. Seismic design categories A–F. Spectral response parameters (SDS, SD1). Ground motion maps.

## NCEER/NSF Workshop (Youd et al., 2001)
**Scope:** Consensus document on SPT- and CPT-based liquefaction evaluation. Defines the simplified procedure: CSR calculation, SPT/CPT corrections, CRR curves, magnitude scaling factors, Kσ and Kα corrections.

## Boulanger & Idriss (2014) — CPT and SPT Based Liquefaction Triggering Procedures
**Scope:** Updated CRR correlations for SPT (N₁)₆₀cs and CPT qc1Ncs. Probabilistic framework. Updated rd, MSF, Kσ relationships. Current state of practice for liquefaction triggering.

## ASTM D6066 — Determining the Normalized Penetration Resistance of Sands for Evaluation of Liquefaction Potential
**Scope:** Procedures for determining (N₁)₆₀ from SPT data including energy measurement (ASTM D4633), overburden correction, rod length, borehole diameter, and sampler corrections.

## ASTM D5778 — Standard Test Method for Electronic Friction Cone and Piezocone Penetration Testing
**Scope:** CPTu procedures. Provides qt, fs, u2 data for liquefaction assessment via Robertson & Wride (1998) or Boulanger & Idriss (2014) CPT methods.

## FHWA GEC-007 — Soil Nail Walls / Ground Improvement (various FHWA manuals)
**Scope:** FHWA guidance on ground improvement methods for liquefaction mitigation including stone columns, deep mixing, compaction grouting, and vibro-compaction.

## USACE ER 1110-2-1806 — Earthquake Design and Evaluation for Civil Works Projects
**Scope:** Seismic evaluation of dams, levees, and other USACE structures. Defines Operating Basis Earthquake (OBE) and Maximum Credible Earthquake (MCE). Liquefaction assessment requirements for embankment dams.

## USBR Design Standards No. 13, Chapter 13 — Seismic Design and Analysis
**Scope:** Seismic evaluation of USBR dams. Pseudostatic methods, Newmark displacement analysis, dynamic effective stress analysis. Liquefaction triggering and post-liquefaction stability assessment.
