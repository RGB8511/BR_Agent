# Standards Reference — Spillway Design

## USBR Design of Small Dams (1987)
**Scope:** Chapters 9–11 cover spillway design: overflow (ogee) crests, chute spillways, side-channel spillways, morning glory spillways, conduit spillways. Includes crest profile equations, discharge coefficients, design charts, and stilling basin design. Despite the title "small dams," the hydraulic principles apply to all dam sizes.

## USBR Design Standards No. 14, Chapter 3 — General Spillway Design
**Scope:** Current USBR design standards for spillways. Supersedes some aspects of Design of Small Dams. Covers spillway classification, design flood requirements, hydraulic design, structural considerations, and instrumentation. Includes guidance on RCC stepped spillways.

## USBR Engineering Monograph No. 25 — Hydraulic Design of Stilling Basins and Energy Dissipators
**Scope:** Comprehensive reference for stilling basin design. USBR Types I through IV, including design charts for basin floor elevation, chute block and baffle pier sizing, end sill design, and tailwater requirements. Also covers hollow-jet valve stilling basins and impact-type dissipators.

## USBR Engineering Monograph No. 42 — Cavitation in Chutes and Spillways (Falvey, 1990)
**Scope:** Cavitation mechanics on hydraulic structures. Cavitation index calculation, surface tolerance specifications, aeration ramp/slot design, and cavitation damage assessment. The definitive reference for spillway cavitation engineering.

## USACE Hydraulic Design Criteria (HDC)
**Scope:** Collection of design sheets organized by topic: Series 111 (spillway crests), Series 112 (chute spillways), Series 120 (stilling basins). Provides graphical design data including C₀ vs He/Hd for various approach conditions, crest pressures, and basin dimensions.

## USACE EM 1110-2-1603 — Hydraulic Design of Spillways
**Scope:** USACE engineering manual for spillway design. Covers crest design, approach conditions, chute flow, training walls, transitions, and energy dissipation. Includes guidance on model testing requirements for major spillways.

## FEMA P-1015 — Technical Manual: Overtopping Protection of Dams
**Scope:** Design guidance for protecting embankment dams against overtopping erosion. Roller-compacted concrete, articulated concrete blocks, reinforced grass, and other overtopping protection systems. Includes design procedures, hydraulic testing data, and construction guidance.

## Tullis et al. (1995) — Design of Labyrinth Spillways
**Scope:** Design methodology for labyrinth weirs. Discharge coefficient relationships as a function of Ht/P, L/W, sidewall angle (α), and crest shape. Laboratory test data for trapezoidal and triangular labyrinth weirs. Extended by Crookston & Tullis (2013).

## ICOLD Bulletin 58 — Spillways for Dams
**Scope:** International overview of spillway types, design principles, and worldwide practice. Classification, selection criteria, and construction considerations. Includes case studies from major dams globally.
