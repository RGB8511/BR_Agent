# Standards Reference — Concrete Testing Methods

## ASTM C39 — Compressive Strength of Cylindrical Concrete Specimens
**Scope:** Standard test for f'c. Cylinders loaded at 0.25 ± 0.05 MPa/s. Requires capping (C617 sulfur or C1231 neoprene). Report strength, fracture pattern, and any defects. Single-operator precision: CV ≈ 3.2% for 150mm cylinders.

## ASTM C78 — Flexural Strength (Third-Point Loading)
**Scope:** Beam loaded at third points — produces constant moment (zero shear) in middle third. If fracture occurs in middle third: f_r = PL/bd². If outside middle third by ≤ 5%: modified formula. Discard if > 5% outside.

## ASTM C293 — Flexural Strength (Center-Point Loading)
**Scope:** Single load at midspan. Produces maximum moment and shear at same location. Gives ~15% higher MOR than third-point. Less preferred because of combined stress state.

## ASTM C496 — Split Tensile Strength
**Scope:** Brazilian test. Cylinder on side, loaded along length through plywood strips. Near-uniform tension across vertical diametral plane. f_t = 2P/(πLD).

## ASTM C469 — Static Modulus of Elasticity and Poisson's Ratio
**Scope:** Compressometer and extensometer on cylinder. Load to 40% f'c, measure longitudinal and lateral strain. Secant modulus from origin to 40% f'c. Requires at least 3 loading cycles (first cycle preconditioning).

## ASTM C42 — Obtaining and Testing Drilled Cores
**Scope:** Core drilling, preparation, and testing procedures. Diameter ≥ 94mm preferred (≥ 3× max aggregate). Soak 40 ± 4 hours before testing (or as specified). Apply L/D correction for L/D < 1.75. ACI 318 acceptance: avg of 3 cores ≥ 0.85f'c, no single core < 0.75f'c.

## ASTM C805 — Rebound Number of Hardened Concrete
**Scope:** Schmidt/rebound hammer. 10 readings at each test location, discard readings > 6 units from median, average remainder. Surface must be smooth, dry, and firmly supported. Not for absolute strength — use for uniformity comparison only.

## ASTM C597 — Pulse Velocity Through Concrete
**Scope:** Ultrasonic pulse timer with 54 kHz transducers. Direct (through), semi-direct, or indirect transmission. Requires coupling agent. Affected by moisture, reinforcement, cracks, aggregates.

## ASTM C1074 — Estimating Concrete Strength by the Maturity Method
**Scope:** Three parts: (1) lab calibration — develop strength-maturity relationship, (2) field instrumentation — embed temperature sensors, (3) estimation — calculate maturity and estimate strength. Requires calibration for each specific mix.

## ASTM C31 — Making and Curing Concrete Test Specimens in the Field
**Scope:** Field-cured and standard-cured cylinders. Standard curing: 16–27°C for 48 hours then lime water at 23 ± 2°C. Field curing: alongside structure for form stripping decisions. Mold types, consolidation methods (rodding vs. vibration based on slump).

## ASTM C617 — Capping Cylindrical Concrete Specimens
**Scope:** Sulfur mortar caps, 3–6 mm thick. Requires planeness ≤ 0.05 mm and perpendicularity to axis within 0.5°.

## ASTM C1231 — Use of Unbonded Caps in Determination of Compressive Strength
**Scope:** Neoprene pad in steel retainers. Pads qualified for specific strength ranges (50 durometer for ≤ 50 MPa, 60 durometer for ≤ 85 MPa). Pads replaced after 100 uses or visible damage.
