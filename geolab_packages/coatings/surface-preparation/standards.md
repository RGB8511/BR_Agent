# Standards Reference — Surface Preparation

## SSPC-SP1 — Solvent Cleaning
**Scope:** Removal of all visible oil, grease, soil, drawing and cutting compounds, and other soluble contaminants. Methods include solvent wipe, immersion, emulsion cleaning, alkaline cleaning, and steam cleaning. Always the first preparation step.

## SSPC-SP5 / NACE No. 1 / ISO 8501-1 Sa 3 — White Metal Blast Cleaning
**Scope:** All mill scale, rust, coating, oxides, and foreign matter removed. Surface has uniform metallic color. Visual standard: SSPC-VIS 1, photographs A/B/C/D Sa 3.

## SSPC-SP10 / NACE No. 2 / ISO 8501-1 Sa 2½ — Near-White Metal Blast Cleaning
**Scope:** At least 95% of each area free of visible residues. Light shadows/streaks on ≤ 5%. Most commonly specified cleanliness level for industrial coatings.

## SSPC-SP6 / NACE No. 3 / ISO 8501-1 Sa 2 — Commercial Blast Cleaning
**Scope:** At least 67% of surface free of visible residues. Staining on ≤ 33%.

## SSPC-SP11 — Power Tool Cleaning to Bare Metal
**Scope:** Remove all visible rust, mill scale, and coating using power tools. Achieve minimum 25 μm (1 mil) angular profile. Rotary impact tools (needle guns, rotary flap wheels) required.

## SSPC-SP12 / NACE No. 5 — Surface Preparation by Water Jetting
**Scope:** Four cleanliness levels (WJ-1 through WJ-4). Classified by pressure and visual result. Does not produce angular profile on bare steel without abrasive injection.

## SSPC-VIS 1 — Guide and Reference Photographs for Steel Surfaces Prepared by Dry Abrasive Blast Cleaning
**Scope:** Color photographs showing initial rust grades (A–D) after preparation to Sa 1, Sa 2, Sa 2½, and Sa 3. The visual acceptance standard for blast-cleaned steel.

## ASTM D4417 — Standard Test Methods for Field Measurement of Surface Profile
**Scope:** Three methods: Method A (comparator), Method B (stylus profilometer), Method C (replica tape / Testex Press-O-Film). Method C is most widely used in field.

## ASTM D4940 — Standard Test Method for Conductimetric Analysis of Water Soluble Ionic Contamination of Blasting Abrasives
**Scope:** Measures soluble salt contamination in blast abrasive. Maximum conductivity limits for recycled and expendable abrasives to prevent salt contamination of surfaces.

## ISO 8502-6 — Extraction of Soluble Contaminants (Bresle Method)
**Scope:** Bresle patch method for extracting soluble salts from steel surfaces. Patch applied, deionized water injected, extracted after dwell time, tested for conductivity or ion content.

## SSPC Guide 15 — Field Methods for Retrieval and Analysis of Soluble Salts on Steel Surfaces
**Scope:** Comprehensive guide for soluble salt testing in the field. Extraction methods (Bresle, swab, sleeve), analysis methods (conductivity, ion-specific), interpretation, and acceptance criteria.
