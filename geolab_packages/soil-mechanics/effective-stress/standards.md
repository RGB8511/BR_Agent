# Standards Reference — Effective Stress & Pore Pressure

## Terzaghi (1943) — Theoretical Soil Mechanics
**Scope:** Principle of effective stress: σ' = σ - u. Foundation of modern soil mechanics. All measurable effects of stress change (strength, volume change) are due exclusively to changes in effective stress.

## Skempton (1954) — The Pore-Pressure Coefficients A and B
**Scope:** Pore pressure parameters A and B relating pore pressure change to applied stress increments: Δu = B[Δσ₃ + A(Δσ₁ - Δσ₃)]. B = 1.0 for saturated soils. A varies with stress history and stress level.

## ASTM D4767 — Standard Test Method for Consolidated Undrained Triaxial Compression Test (CU)
**Scope:** Pore pressure measurement during shear in triaxial testing. Provides effective stress parameters c' and φ', pore pressure parameter A at failure.

## ASTM D2435 — Standard Test Method for One-Dimensional Consolidation Properties of Soils
**Scope:** Oedometer test with pore pressure dissipation. Measures consolidation behavior under known effective stress increments. Back-calculates in-situ effective stress history (σ'p).

## ASTM D2850 — Standard Test Method for Unconsolidated-Undrained Triaxial Compression Test
**Scope:** UU test on saturated cohesive soils. No drainage permitted. Total stress analysis. φ = 0 concept for saturated clay under undrained loading.

## Bishop & Henkel (1962) — The Measurement of Soil Properties in the Triaxial Test
**Scope:** Comprehensive reference for pore pressure measurement techniques, saturation procedures (back-pressure saturation to B ≥ 0.95), and interpretation of effective stress paths.
