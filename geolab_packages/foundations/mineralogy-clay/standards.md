# Standards Reference — Mineralogy & Clay Behavior

## ASTM D4647 — Standard Test Methods for Identification and Classification of Dispersive Clay Soils by the Pinhole Test

**Scope:** Direct measurement of clay dispersivity by passing water through a 1.0 mm diameter hole punched through a compacted specimen.

**Procedure:** Apply hydraulic head in stages (50, 180, 360, 1020 mm). Observe effluent clarity (dark = colloidal clay in suspension), flow rate, and hole enlargement. Classify D1/D2 (dispersive), ND4/ND3 (intermediate), ND2/ND1 (non-dispersive).

**Key Notes:**
- Most reliable single test for dispersivity
- Use distilled water (not tap water — dissolved salts suppress dispersion)
- Test at natural moisture content when possible; remolding alters fabric

## ASTM D4221 — Standard Test Methods for Dispersive Characteristics of Clay Soil by Double Hydrometer

**Scope:** Compares hydrometer results with and without chemical dispersant (sodium hexametaphosphate).

**Dispersion Ratio:** % fines (no dispersant) / % fines (with dispersant) × 100. DR > 50% = dispersive; 30–50% = intermediate; < 30% = non-dispersive.

**Key Notes:**
- Simpler and faster than pinhole test
- Less reliable as sole indicator — use in combination with pinhole and chemical tests
- Can produce false negatives for some dispersive soils

## ASTM D4546 — Standard Test Methods for One-Dimensional Swell or Collapse of Soils

**Scope:** Measurement of swell potential, swell pressure, and collapse of soils in an oedometer.

**Methods:**
- **Method A (wetting after loading):** Load specimen to in-situ stress, then inundate. Measure swell or collapse.
- **Method B (single point swell pressure):** Inundate specimen at seating load, allow to swell, then consolidate back to original height. The pressure at original height = swell pressure.
- **Method C (loaded swell, multiple specimens):** Multiple specimens loaded to different stresses before inundation. Generates swell vs. pressure relationship.

**Key Notes:**
- Constant-volume method gives swell pressure directly
- Swell depends strongly on initial moisture content and density
- Specimen must represent field compaction conditions
