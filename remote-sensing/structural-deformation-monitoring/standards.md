# Standards Reference — Structural Deformation Monitoring

## ISRM Suggested Methods for Rock Mass Monitoring (2012)
**Scope:** International Society for Rock Mechanics suggested methods for monitoring displacement, convergence, and deformation in rock masses. Covers geodetic, photogrammetric, and remote sensing methods including TLS. Provides guidance on monitoring network design and data interpretation.

## ICOLD Bulletin 158 — Dam Surveillance (2018)
**Scope:** International Commission on Large Dams guidance on surveillance including geodetic monitoring, instrumentation, and remote sensing. Covers monitoring objectives, measurement frequency, data management, and threshold-based response protocols.

## USACE EM 1110-2-1009 — Structural Deformation Surveying
**Scope:** US Army Corps of Engineers manual for deformation surveying of civil works structures. Geodetic network design, measurement techniques, analysis methods, and reporting requirements for dams, locks, levees, and buildings.

## ISO 17123 — Field Procedures for Testing Geodetic Instruments
**Scope:** Standardized field test procedures for surveying instruments including total stations and laser scanners. Part 9 covers TLS. Used to verify instrument performance for monitoring applications.

## Lague et al. (2013) — M3C2 Algorithm Paper
**Scope:** Journal of Geophysical Research publication defining the M3C2 distance algorithm with spatially variable confidence intervals. The peer-reviewed reference for point cloud change detection methodology.

## FIG Publication 43 — Monitoring of Structures (2015)
**Scope:** International Federation of Surveyors guide to structural monitoring. Measurement systems, accuracy requirements, network design, deformation analysis, and reporting for buildings, bridges, dams, and tunnels.
