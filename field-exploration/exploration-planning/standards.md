# Standards Reference — Exploration Planning

## FHWA GEC 5 — Geotechnical Site Characterization (2017)
**Scope:** Comprehensive FHWA guidance (Geotechnical Engineering Circular No. 5) on planning and executing geotechnical investigations for highway projects. Covers investigation phases, boring layout and depth, in-situ and lab testing, geophysical methods, and data interpretation. The primary US reference for investigation planning.

## FHWA NHI-01-031 — Subsurface Investigations (Mayne et al., 2002)
**Scope:** FHWA training manual on geotechnical site characterization. Covers drilling methods, sampling, in-situ testing (SPT, CPT, DMT, PMT, vane), geophysics, lab testing, and interpretation. Companion to GEC 5 with more detailed coverage of individual methods.

## USACE EM 1110-1-1804 — Geotechnical Investigations
**Scope:** USACE guidance on planning and executing geotechnical investigations for all civil works (dams, levees, channels, structures). Covers investigation scope by project type, drilling and sampling, in-situ testing, lab testing, and reporting requirements. Specifies minimum investigation requirements for USACE projects.

## ASTM D420 — Investigating and Sampling Soil and Rock
**Scope:** Standard guide for conducting surface and subsurface investigations. Covers reconnaissance, investigation planning, drilling, sampling, in-situ testing, and groundwater measurements. Framework standard that references specific method standards.

## ASTM D2487 — Classification of Soils for Engineering Purposes (USCS)
**Scope:** Laboratory classification of soils using the Unified Soil Classification System. Based on grain size distribution and Atterberg limits. Defines group symbols (GW, GP, GM, GC, SW, SP, SM, SC, ML, CL, OL, MH, CH, OH, Pt) and group names. The standard engineering soil classification in North America.

## ASTM D2488 — Description and Identification of Soils (Visual-Manual Procedure)
**Scope:** Field classification of soils by visual-manual procedures without laboratory testing. Includes estimating percentages of gravel, sand, and fines; field plasticity tests; and standard descriptors for moisture, density/consistency, color, and structure.

## AASHTO R13 — Conducting Geotechnical Subsurface Investigations
**Scope:** AASHTO recommended practice for transportation project investigations. Specifies minimum boring requirements for bridges, retaining walls, roadways, and embankments. Referenced by most state DOTs.

## Eurocode 7 Part 2 — Ground Investigation and Testing (EN 1997-2)
**Scope:** European standard for geotechnical investigation. Specifies requirements for investigation scope, quality, and reporting. Categories of investigation (1, 2, 3) based on geotechnical complexity. Complementary to national annexes.
