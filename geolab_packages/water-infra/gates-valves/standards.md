# Standards Reference — Gates, Valves & Flow Control

## USACE EM 1110-2-1603 — Hydraulic Design of Spillways
**Scope:** USACE guidance for spillway hydraulic design including gated spillways. Discharge coefficients for radial and vertical-lift gates, gate operating procedures, approach conditions, and pier effects. Includes Hydraulic Design Charts (HDC) for standard configurations.

## USACE EM 1110-2-1602 — Hydraulic Design of Reservoir Outlet Works
**Scope:** USACE guidance for outlet works design. Intake design, conduit hydraulics, gate/valve selection, air demand, cavitation analysis, and energy dissipation. Comprehensive treatment of high-head gates and valves.

## USBR Design Standards No. 14, Chapter 3 — General Spillway Design Considerations
**Scope:** USBR standards for spillway gates including radial gates, vertical-lift gates, and drum gates. Structural design, trunnion design, seal design, hoist systems, and operating criteria.

## AWWA C500 — Metal-Seated Gate Valves for Water Supply Service
**Scope:** AWWA standard for gate valves 3–48 inches. Materials, design, testing, and marking. For isolation service (not regulation).

## AWWA C504 — Rubber-Seated Butterfly Valves, 3 In. Through 72 In.
**Scope:** AWWA standard for butterfly valves. Design, materials, testing, and marking. Covers standard (150 psi) and high-pressure (250 psi) classes.

## AWWA C507 — Ball Valves, 6 In. Through 60 In.
**Scope:** AWWA standard for ball valves for water service. Design, materials, torque requirements, and testing.

## Lewin (2001) — Hydraulic Gates and Valves in Free Surface Flow and Submerged Outlets
**Scope:** Comprehensive text on gate and valve hydraulics. Covers all major gate types, discharge coefficients, forces, vibration, cavitation, and design procedures. Includes worked examples and design charts.

## USACE Hydraulic Design Criteria (HDC)
**Scope:** Collection of design charts for hydraulic structures developed from USACE model studies. Includes discharge coefficients, pressure distributions, and forces for standard gate configurations. Now incorporated into EM series but still referenced independently.
