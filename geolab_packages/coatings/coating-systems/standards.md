# Standards Reference — Protective Coating Systems

## ISO 12944 — Corrosion Protection of Steel Structures by Protective Paint Systems
**Scope:** Comprehensive 9-part international standard. Part 2: corrosivity categories (C1–CX). Part 4: surface preparation types. Part 5: coating systems by category and durability (Low/Medium/High/Very High). Part 9: offshore and related structures.

## SSPC-PA 2 — Procedure for Determining Conformance to Dry Coating Thickness Requirements
**Scope:** Defines how to measure and evaluate DFT compliance. Specifies spot measurements (average of 3 gauge readings), area measurements, and acceptance criteria. Gauge calibration requirements per SSPC-PA 9.

## SSPC Surface Preparation Standards (SP 1–SP 16)
**Scope:** Progressive cleanliness levels from solvent cleaning (SP 1) through white metal blast (SP 5). Joint standards with NACE. SP 1 (solvent clean) is always required first. Visual standards: SSPC-VIS 1 (blast), SSPC-VIS 3 (power/hand tool), SSPC-VIS 4/5 (water jetting).

## ASTM D4541 — Pull-Off Strength of Coatings Using Portable Adhesion Testers
**Scope:** Bond a dolly with adhesive, pull to failure. Reports adhesion in MPa and failure mode. Type I–V testers specified. Critical: score around dolly perimeter before pulling to isolate test area.

## ASTM D3359 — Measuring Adhesion by Tape Test
**Scope:** Method A (X-cut): single X-cut, apply tape, rate peeling. Method B (cross-cut): grid pattern with multi-blade cutter, apply tape, rate removal. Quick field test but less quantitative than D4541.

## NACE SP0188 — Discontinuity (Holiday) Testing of New Protective Coatings on Conductive Substrates
**Scope:** Low-voltage wet sponge (< 500 μm coatings) and high-voltage spark (≥ 500 μm coatings). Defines voltage calculation, test speed, electrode types, and marking of holidays. All holidays must be repaired and retested.

## ASTM D5162 — Discontinuity (Holiday) Testing of Nonconductive Protective Coating on Metallic Substrates
**Scope:** Similar to NACE SP0188 with more detail on high-voltage DC testing. Voltage selection, operator speed (≤ 0.3 m/s), and reporting requirements.

## SSPC-SP 5/NACE No. 1 — White Metal Blast Cleaning
**Scope:** Remove all visible rust, mill scale, paint, and foreign matter. Most stringent blast standard. Required for IOZ and immersion service. Surface must appear uniformly metallic gray.

## SSPC-SP 10/NACE No. 2 — Near-White Metal Blast Cleaning
**Scope:** At least 95% of surface free of all visible residue. Light shadows, streaks, or discoloration on ≤ 5% of area. Standard for most high-performance primer systems.

## ASTM D4414 — Measurement of Wet Film Thickness by Notch Gauges
**Scope:** Wet film thickness measured immediately after application. Notch gauge pressed into wet film — highest notch wetted indicates WFT. Used to calculate expected DFT during application.
