# Standards Reference — Slope Stability & Mass Movement Monitoring

## ISRM Suggested Methods — Rock Slope Monitoring Using Remote Sensing
**Scope:** International Society for Rock Mechanics guidance on applying LiDAR, photogrammetry, InSAR, and GB-InSAR to rock slope monitoring. Covers acquisition, processing, change detection, and integration with geotechnical analysis. Recommended procedures for discontinuity mapping and slope displacement measurement.

## JTC-1 (Joint Technical Committee on Landslides) Guidelines
**Scope:** ISSMGE/IAEG/ISRM joint committee guidelines on landslide investigation, monitoring, and early warning. Covers remote sensing integration with traditional monitoring, alert threshold development, and emergency response frameworks.

## USGS Landslide Hazards Program — Monitoring Methods
**Scope:** US Geological Survey guidance and technical reports on landslide monitoring including InSAR, LiDAR change detection, and GPS. Open-file reports provide case studies and methodological guidance. USGS operates continuous GPS and InSAR monitoring networks on several critical landslides.

## AGS (Australian Geomechanics Society) — Landslide Risk Management Guidelines
**Scope:** Comprehensive framework for landslide risk assessment and management. Includes guidance on monitoring methods, alert levels, and acceptable risk criteria. Remote sensing increasingly integrated into the monitoring framework.

## Hungr et al. (2014) — Varnes Classification of Landslide Types (Updated)
**Scope:** Updated classification of landslide types (fall, topple, slide, spread, flow) with velocity classes. Essential reference for characterizing monitored mass movements. Each type has different remote sensing signatures and monitoring requirements.

## Abellán et al. (2014) — Terrestrial Laser Scanning of Rock Slope Instabilities
**Scope:** Comprehensive review of TLS application to rock slope monitoring. Covers point cloud comparison methods, rockfall detection and quantification, pre-failure deformation measurement, and multi-temporal analysis. Practical guidance on scanner placement, registration, and error assessment.

## Carlà et al. (2017) — Guidelines for the Use of Inverse Velocity Method for Landslide Failure Time Estimation
**Scope:** Practical methodology for applying the Fukuzono inverse velocity approach to GB-InSAR and InSAR displacement time series. Discusses limitations, filtering requirements, and uncertainty in failure time prediction. Essential for operational early warning systems.
