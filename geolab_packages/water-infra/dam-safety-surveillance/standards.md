# Standards Reference — Dam Safety & Surveillance

## FEMA 93 — Federal Guidelines for Dam Safety
**Scope:** Framework document for the National Dam Safety Program. Establishes principles for dam safety including hazard classification, inspection, EAPs, and regulatory responsibilities. Foundational policy document.

## FEMA 145 — Dam Safety: An Owner's Guidance Manual
**Scope:** Practical guidance for dam owners on their responsibilities. Covers inspections, maintenance, EAPs, regulatory compliance, and when to engage professional engineers. Accessible to non-technical dam owners.

## FERC Engineering Guidelines, Chapter 14 — Dam Safety Surveillance Monitoring Plan
**Scope:** FERC requirements for surveillance monitoring at licensed hydropower dams. Instrument selection, reading frequency, data management, threshold levels, and reporting requirements. The most detailed US federal guidance on dam instrumentation programs.

## USACE ER 1110-2-1156 — Safety of Dams — Policy and Procedures
**Scope:** USACE dam safety regulation covering inspection, evaluation, risk assessment, and remediation of USACE dams. Includes PFMA, risk assessment, and tolerable risk guidelines.

## ICOLD Bulletin 68 — Monitoring of Dams and Their Foundations
**Scope:** International guidance on dam monitoring. Instrument types, layout, reading frequency, data processing, and interpretation. Covers embankment, concrete gravity, arch, and buttress dams.

## ICOLD Bulletin 118 — Relations Between Dam Safety and Dam Monitoring
**Scope:** ICOLD guidance on using monitoring data for safety assessment. Statistical analysis methods (HST model), anomaly detection, and decision frameworks for safety actions.

## Dunnicliff (1993) — Geotechnical Instrumentation for Monitoring Field Performance
**Scope:** The definitive reference on geotechnical instrumentation. Covers all instrument types (piezometers, inclinometers, extensometers, settlement devices, load cells), selection, installation, calibration, data collection, and interpretation. Applicable to dams, embankments, tunnels, and slopes.

## FERC Part 12D — Independent Consultant Review
**Scope:** FERC regulation requiring periodic independent engineering review of licensed hydropower dams. Comprehensive safety evaluation covering stability, hydrology, seismicity, instrumentation, and operations. Required every 5 years for high/significant hazard dams.
