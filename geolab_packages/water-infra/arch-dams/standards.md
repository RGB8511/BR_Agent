# Standards Reference — Arch Dams

## USBR Design Standards No. 14, Chapter 4 — Arch Dams
**Scope:** USBR criteria for arch dam design. Includes layout geometry, load combinations, stress criteria, stability analysis, foundation requirements, and instrumentation. References the USBR trial load method and finite element analysis procedures.

## USBR — Design of Arch Dams (1977)
**Scope:** Classic USBR publication on arch dam design methodology. Covers thin cylinder preliminary design, independent arch analysis, trial load method, layout optimization, thermal analysis, and construction considerations. Still widely referenced for the trial load method.

## FERC Engineering Guidelines, Chapter 11 — Arch Dams
**Scope:** FERC dam safety criteria for existing arch dams. Visual inspection, static analysis (stress and stability), dynamic (seismic) analysis, potential failure mode analysis, and evaluation criteria. Defines stress limits and stability factors of safety for usual, unusual, and extreme loading.

## USACE EM 1110-2-2201 — Arch Dam Design
**Scope:** USACE engineering manual for arch dam analysis and design. Layout and geometry, load combinations, trial load and finite element methods, foundation analysis, thermal analysis, and construction requirements.

## ICOLD Bulletin 155 — Guidelines for Use of Numerical Models in Dam Engineering
**Scope:** ICOLD guidance on finite element and other numerical modeling for dams. Covers model validation, mesh refinement, constitutive models for concrete and rock, dam-foundation interaction, seismic analysis, and quality assurance. Essential for modern arch dam FEM analysis.

## ICOLD Bulletin 168 — Recommendations for Operation, Maintenance and Rehabilitation of Large Dams
**Scope:** Includes guidance on monitoring and evaluating arch dams during operation. Deformation monitoring (geodetic surveys, pendulums, extensometers), seepage, joint behavior, and trigger values for intervention.

## ACI 207.1R — Guide to Mass Concrete
**Scope:** While not specific to arch dams, provides essential guidance on thermal analysis, concrete properties for mass pours, temperature control, and contraction joint design that directly applies to arch dam concrete.
