# Standards Reference — Field Density Testing

## ASTM D6938 — Standard Test Methods for In-Place Density and Water Content of Soil and Soil-Aggregate by Nuclear Methods (Shallow Depth)
**Scope:** Nuclear density gauge (NDG) procedures for measuring in-place wet density, dry density, and moisture content. Covers direct transmission and backscatter modes. Includes calibration, standardization, and reporting requirements. Most widely used field density test method.

## ASTM D1556 — Standard Test Method for Density and Unit Weight of Soil in Place by Sand-Cone Method
**Scope:** Sand replacement method for determining in-place density. Covers apparatus (cone, jar, base plate, calibrated sand), sand calibration, test hole excavation, volume determination, and calculations. Reference method for verifying NDG results.

## ASTM D2167 — Standard Test Method for Density and Unit Weight of Soil in Place by the Rubber Balloon Method
**Scope:** Volume replacement using water-filled balloon. Alternative to sand cone. Covers apparatus, calibration, procedure, and calculations.

## ASTM D2937 — Standard Test Method for Density of Soil in Place by the Drive-Cylinder Method
**Scope:** Thin-walled cylinder driven into soil; density from mass and known volume. For cohesive soils only. Simple and fast but limited applicability.

## ASTM D698 — Standard Test Methods for Laboratory Compaction Characteristics of Soil Using Standard Effort
**Scope:** Standard Proctor test. 5.5-lb hammer, 12-in drop, 3 layers, 25 blows. Determines maximum dry density and optimum moisture content at standard compactive effort (600 kN·m/m³).

## ASTM D1557 — Standard Test Methods for Laboratory Compaction Characteristics of Soil Using Modified Effort
**Scope:** Modified Proctor test. 10-lb hammer, 18-in drop, 5 layers, 25 blows. Higher compactive effort (2700 kN·m/m³) produces higher maximum dry density at lower optimum moisture. Reference for highway and structural fill specifications.

## ASTM D4253 / D4254 — Maximum / Minimum Index Density of Soils Using a Vibratory Table / Funnel
**Scope:** Maximum and minimum density (void ratio) of granular soils for relative density calculation. D4253 uses vibratory table for densest state; D4254 uses funnel or inverted mold for loosest state. For soils with ≤ 12% fines.

## ASTM D6758 — Standard Test Method for Measuring Stiffness and Apparent Modulus of Soil and Soil-Aggregate In-Place by Electro-Mechanical Method of Operating Circular Plate
**Scope:** Humboldt GeoGauge procedure. Measures surface stiffness and modulus of upper ~225 mm. Not a direct density measurement but correlates with compaction quality. Rapid QA screening tool.
