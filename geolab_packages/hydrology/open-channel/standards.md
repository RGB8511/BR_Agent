# Standards Reference — Open Channel Hydraulics

## Chow (1959) — Open Channel Hydraulics
**Scope:** The definitive textbook reference. Manning's equation, uniform flow, gradually and rapidly varied flow, unsteady flow, design of channels. Manning's n tables are the most comprehensive compilation available.

## USACE HEC-RAS — Hydrologic Engineering Center's River Analysis System
**Scope:** Standard software for open channel hydraulics. Steady and unsteady 1-D and 2-D flow analysis. Water surface profiles, floodway encroachment, bridge/culvert hydraulics, dam breach, sediment transport. The standard of practice for floodplain analysis.

## USACE EM 1110-2-1601 — Hydraulic Design of Flood Control Channels
**Scope:** Design of improved channels for flood control. Manning's n selection, freeboard, permissible velocities, riprap sizing, supercritical flow channels, channel transitions, and energy dissipators.

## FHWA HDS-5 — Hydraulic Design of Highway Culverts
**Scope:** Comprehensive culvert design methodology. Inlet control and outlet control headwater computation. Performance curves, outlet velocity, energy dissipation. Nomographs and equations for circular, box, arch, and pipe-arch culverts.

## FHWA HEC-14 — Hydraulic Design of Energy Dissipators for Culverts and Channels
**Scope:** Design of stilling basins (USBR Types I–IV), riprap basins, impact basins, and drop structures for energy dissipation at culvert outlets and channel transitions.

## FHWA HEC-22 — Urban Drainage Design Manual
**Scope:** Inlet design, storm drain systems, gutter flow, energy and hydraulic grade line calculations for urban storm drainage.

## ISO 1438 — Measurement of Liquid Flow in Open Channels — Thin-Plate Weirs
**Scope:** International standard for flow measurement using rectangular and V-notch (triangular) thin-plate weirs. Discharge coefficients, installation requirements, and accuracy.

## USBR Water Measurement Manual (3rd ed.)
**Scope:** Comprehensive reference for flow measurement in open channels. Weirs, flumes (Parshall, cutthroat), orifices, current meters, acoustic methods. Standard for irrigation and water management.
