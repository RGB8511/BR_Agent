# Standards Reference — Reservoir Planning & Design

## USBR Design of Small Dams (1987) — Chapter 2
**Scope:** Reservoir planning chapter covering site selection, storage requirements, yield analysis, sedimentation, evaporation losses, and area-capacity-elevation curves. Includes Rippl mass curve method and simplified yield procedures.

## USACE EM 1110-2-3600 — Management of Water Control Systems
**Scope:** USACE guidance on reservoir operations. Water control plans, rule curves, real-time operations, flood control operations, and system analysis. Covers multipurpose reservoir management and coordination of reservoir systems.

## USACE ER 1110-2-1941 — Drought Contingency Plans
**Scope:** Requirements for drought planning at USACE reservoirs. Trigger levels, water supply shortage management, and coordination with water supply customers during drought.

## USACE EM 1110-2-4000 — Sedimentation Investigations of Rivers and Reservoirs
**Scope:** Comprehensive guidance on sediment analysis. Watershed sediment yield estimation, reservoir sedimentation surveys (range lines, bathymetry), trap efficiency (Brune curves), sediment distribution (Borland-Miller), and sediment management strategies (flushing, sluicing, dredging).

## USBR Design Standards No. 13, Chapter 6 — Freeboard
**Scope:** Freeboard determination for embankment dams. Wind analysis, wave height and runup computation (SMB method), wind setup, and combined freeboard criteria. Specifies minimum freeboard for normal and maximum reservoir levels.

## USBR Flood Hydrology Manual (1989)
**Scope:** USBR methodology for hydrologic studies supporting dam design. Flood frequency analysis, PMF development, and reservoir routing. Includes guidelines for establishing area-capacity curves from topographic data and reservoir water balance studies.

## USACE Hydrologic Engineering Center — HEC-ResSim
**Scope:** USACE reservoir simulation software. Models reservoir operations including rule curves, outlet capacity, flood control operations, water supply releases, and system operations. Used for operational planning, real-time decision support, and water control plan development.
