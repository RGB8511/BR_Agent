# Standards Reference — Phase Relationships & Index Properties

## ASTM D2216 — Standard Test Methods for Laboratory Determination of Water (Moisture) Content of Soil and Rock by Mass

**Scope:** Gravimetric determination of water content by oven drying at 110 ± 5°C to constant mass.

**Key Notes:**
- Drying time: typically 12–16 hours; check constant mass (< 0.1% change in 1 hour)
- For soils with gypsum or significant organic matter, use reduced temperature (60°C) to prevent loss of structural water or combustion. Report temperature used.
- Microwave drying (D4643) is acceptable for rapid field results but oven method is the reference standard.
- Minimum specimen mass: 20g for fine-grained soils, increasing with maximum particle size.

## ASTM D854 — Standard Test Methods for Specific Gravity of Soil Solids by Pycnometer

**Scope:** Determination of G_s using a calibrated pycnometer (volumetric flask) and de-aired water.

**Key Procedure:**
1. Calibrate pycnometer with de-aired water at test temperature
2. Add oven-dried soil (Method A) or moist soil (Method B) to pycnometer
3. Remove entrapped air (vacuum, boiling, or mechanical agitation)
4. Fill with de-aired water, measure total mass
5. Calculate G_s from mass relationships with temperature correction

**Key Notes:**
- Temperature correction is critical — water density varies with temperature
- Complete de-airing is the primary source of error. Vacuum method preferred.
- Method B (moist specimen) avoids potential alteration of organic soils or clays by oven-drying
- Report to nearest 0.01

## ASTM D4253 — Standard Test Methods for Maximum Index Density and Unit Weight of Soils Using a Vibratory Table

**Scope:** Determination of minimum void ratio (e_min) / maximum dry unit weight (γ_d,max) for cohesionless soils.

**Key Notes:**
- Uses vibratory table method (dry or wet)
- Applicable to soils with ≤ 15% fines and no particles larger than 3 inches
- Wet method preferred for soils with 5–15% fines to prevent segregation

## ASTM D4254 — Standard Test Methods for Minimum Index Density and Unit Weight of Soils and Calculation of Relative Density

**Scope:** Determination of maximum void ratio (e_max) / minimum dry unit weight (γ_d,min) and calculation of D_r.

**Key Notes:**
- Uses funnel pouring method (dry deposition)
- Same particle size and fines content restrictions as D4253
- D_r calculation from D4253/D4254 results plus in-situ density

## ASTM D2937 — Standard Test Method for Density of Soil in Place by the Drive-Cylinder Method

**Scope:** In-situ density by driving a thin-walled cylinder into cohesive or cemented soils.

**Key Notes:**
- Cylinder must be thin-walled to minimize disturbance
- Not suitable for very stiff, gravelly, or loose granular soils
- Alternative to sand cone or nuclear gauge for cohesive soils

## ASTM D7263 — Standard Test Methods for Laboratory Determination of Density and Unit Weight of Soil Specimens

**Scope:** Laboratory measurement of density/unit weight on undisturbed or compacted specimens using direct measurement, wax-coating, or water displacement methods.
