# Standards Reference — CPT / CPTU

## ASTM D5778 — Standard Test Method for Electronic Friction Cone and Piezocone Penetration Testing of Soils
**Scope:** Definitive North American standard for CPT and CPTU. Cone specifications (10 cm² and 15 cm² cones), penetration rate (20 mm/s ± 5), data acquisition requirements, calibration procedures, pore pressure element saturation, and reporting. Covers both mechanical and electronic cones but electronic cones are standard practice.

## ISSMGE Reference Test Procedure for Cone Penetration Test (CPT/CPTU)
**Scope:** International reference procedure by TC-16. Largely consistent with ASTM D5778 but with additional guidance on temperature compensation, inclination monitoring, and quality assurance. Specifies net area ratio calibration, baseline readings, and zero-drift checks.

## ASTM D7400 — Standard Test Methods for Downhole Seismic Testing (for SCPT)
**Scope:** Covers the downhole seismic test method used in SCPT. Source at surface, receiver in cone. Measurement of P-wave and S-wave arrival times for Vs profiling. Used in conjunction with D5778 when SCPT module is deployed.

## Robertson (2009, 2016) — Interpretation Guides
**Key publications:** "Interpretation of Cone Penetration Tests — A Unified Approach" (2009, CGJ). "Cone Penetration Test (CPT)-Based Soil Behaviour Type (SBT) Classification System — An Update" (2016). Defines the SBTn chart, Ic calculation, normalized parameters (Qtn, Fr, Bq), and correlations to engineering properties. The standard-of-practice interpretation framework.

## Lunne, Robertson & Powell (1997) — Cone Penetration Testing in Geotechnical Practice
**Scope:** The definitive textbook on CPT. Equipment, procedures, interpretation methods, soil classification, correlations to Su, φ', OCR, Dr, modulus, consolidation properties, and liquefaction assessment. Essential reference for any CPT practitioner.

## Boulanger & Idriss (2014) — CPT and SPT Based Liquefaction Triggering Procedures
**Scope:** Updated liquefaction evaluation framework using CPT data. Clean-sand base curve, fines correction via Ic, magnitude scaling factors, overburden correction, and probabilistic relationships. Supersedes Youd et al. (2001) for CPT-based liquefaction triggering.

## Mayne (2007) — NCHRP Synthesis 368: Cone Penetration Testing
**Scope:** NCHRP synthesis of CPT practice for transportation applications. Comprehensive summary of correlations for all soil types, equipment considerations, and practical implementation guidance for DOT projects.

## Robertson & Cabal (2015) — Guide to Cone Penetration Testing (Gregg Drilling)
**Scope:** Practical field guide to CPT interpretation. Includes Robertson SBTn charts, worked examples, correlation summaries, and quick-reference tables. Widely used as a desk reference.
