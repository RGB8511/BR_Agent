# Standards Reference — Water Distribution Systems

## 10 States Standards (GLUMRB — Recommended Standards for Water Works)
**Scope:** Minimum design standards for public water systems adopted by most US states. Distribution system requirements: minimum pipe sizes (6 in with hydrants), minimum pressures (20 psi fire flow, 35 psi normal), valve spacing, hydrant placement, dead-end mains, storage requirements, and cross-connection control. The baseline standard for water distribution design in the US.

## AWWA M31 — Distribution System Requirements for Fire Protection (5th ed.)
**Scope:** Design of distribution systems for fire protection capability. Needed fire flow determination, distribution system capacity analysis, hydrant spacing and installation, fire flow testing procedures, and coordination with fire departments and ISO rating. Essential for designing systems that meet fire protection requirements.

## AWWA M32 — Computer Modeling of Water Distribution Systems (4th ed.)
**Scope:** AWWA manual on hydraulic modeling of distribution networks. Model building, calibration (C-factor tests, hydrant flow tests, SCADA data), demand allocation, steady-state and extended-period simulation, fire flow analysis, water quality modeling, and model applications in master planning, design, and operations.

## EPA EPANET
**Scope:** Public-domain hydraulic and water quality modeling software. Solves pipe network hydraulics (demand-driven and pressure-dependent), extended-period simulation, water age, chlorine decay, source tracing, and tank mixing models. Reference: Rossman (2000) EPANET 2 Users Manual. Basis for most commercial water distribution modeling packages.

## AWWA C500 Series — Valves and Hydrants
**Scope:** Standards for distribution system appurtenances:
- **C500:** Metal-Seated Gate Valves (3–48 in)
- **C504:** Rubber-Seated Butterfly Valves (3–72 in)
- **C509:** Resilient-Seated Gate Valves (3–12 in)
- **C510:** Double Check Valve Backflow Prevention Assembly
- **C502:** Dry-Barrel Fire Hydrants
- **C503:** Wet-Barrel Fire Hydrants

## NFPA 24 — Standard for the Installation of Private Fire Service Mains
**Scope:** Private fire service main design and installation. Pipe materials, minimum sizes, hydrant spacing, valve placement, and testing requirements for fire protection water supply on private property.

## AWWA C651 — Disinfecting Water Mains
**Scope:** Procedures for disinfection of new and repaired water mains before placing in service. Tablet, continuous-feed, and slug methods. Chlorine dosing, contact time, flushing, and bacteriological testing requirements.

## IWA Water Loss Best Practice Manuals
**Scope:** International Water Association guidance on water loss management. Standard water balance methodology (IWA/AWWA), real loss component analysis, Infrastructure Leakage Index (ILI), pressure management, active leakage control, and economic level of leakage. The international standard for water loss assessment and reduction.
