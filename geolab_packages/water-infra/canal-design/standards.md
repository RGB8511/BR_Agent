# Standards Reference — Canal Design

## USBR Design Standards No. 3, Chapter 1 — Canals and Related Structures
**Scope:** Comprehensive USBR design criteria for canals. Hydraulic design (Manning's, tractive force), cross-section geometry, freeboard, transitions, lining selection, structural design of lining, and appurtenances. The primary US federal standard for irrigation and water conveyance canal design.

## USBR Design of Small Canal Structures (1978)
**Scope:** Practical design manual for canal structures including transitions, checks, drops, turnouts, division structures, flumes, inverted siphons, and road crossings. Includes standardized designs for structures up to about 3 m³/s capacity.

## USACE EM 1110-2-1601 — Hydraulic Design of Flood Control Channels
**Scope:** USACE guidance for design of flood control channels. Channel stability (tractive force, permissible velocity), riprap design, channel transitions, supercritical flow channels, and composite lining design. Applicable to large drainage and conveyance channels.

## Chow (1959) — Open-Channel Hydraulics
**Scope:** The definitive textbook on open-channel hydraulics. Uniform flow (Manning's), gradually varied flow profiles, rapidly varied flow (hydraulic jump), channel design, and unsteady flow. Foundational reference for all canal hydraulic design.

## IS 10430 — Criteria for Design of Lined Canals and Guidance for Design of Canals in Expansive Soils
**Scope:** Indian Standard for lined canal design widely used internationally. Design criteria for concrete, brick, and membrane-lined canals including structural design of lining, joint spacing, subgrade drainage, and protection against expansive soils.

## FAO Irrigation and Drainage Paper 26 — Irrigation Canal Lining (Kraatz)
**Scope:** FAO comprehensive guide to canal lining. Lining types (concrete, shotcrete, membrane, earth, asphalt), seepage reduction effectiveness, cost comparison, construction methods, and maintenance. International perspective with tropical/arid region experience.
