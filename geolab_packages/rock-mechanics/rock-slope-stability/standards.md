# Standards Reference — Rock Slope Stability

## Wyllie & Mah (2004) — Rock Slope Engineering (4th ed.)
**Scope:** Comprehensive reference for rock slope analysis and design. Kinematic analysis, planar/wedge/toppling failure modes, limit equilibrium methods, probabilistic analysis, stabilization measures.

## Hoek & Bray (1981) — Rock Slope Engineering (3rd ed.)
**Scope:** Classic reference. Stereonet methods, Markland test, planar and wedge analysis equations, water pressure effects, rockfall trajectories, and design charts.

## FHWA-IF-99-025 — Rock Slopes Reference Manual
**Scope:** Design and construction of rock slopes for highways. Includes controlled blasting, scaling, rock reinforcement (bolts, anchors, shotcrete), catchment design (Ritchie ditches), draped mesh, and monitoring.

## ASTM D5731 — Standard Test Method for Determination of the Point Load Strength Index
**Scope:** Point load testing for intact rock strength estimation. Diametral and axial tests on core, irregular lumps. Size correction to Is(50). Correlation to UCS: σc ≈ (20–25) × Is(50).

## ISRM Suggested Methods for Rock Characterization
**Scope:** Discontinuity surveys (scanline, window mapping), orientation measurement, persistence, roughness (JRC profiles), aperture, infilling, and weathering description.

## Ritchie (1963) — Evaluation of Rockfall and Its Control
**Scope:** Original research on rockfall trajectories and catchment ditch design. Ritchie ditch width and depth criteria based on slope height and angle. Still widely referenced for highway rock cut design.
