# Standards Reference — Slope Stability

## USACE EM 1110-2-1902 — Slope Stability
**Scope:** Comprehensive USACE guidance for slope stability analysis. Method selection (Bishop, Spencer, Morgenstern-Price), required FS by loading condition (end-of-construction, steady-state seepage, rapid drawdown, seismic). FS requirements: 1.3 (normal), 1.1 (rapid drawdown), 1.0 (earthquake + Newmark displacement check).

## FHWA-NHI-11-032 — Geotechnical Engineering Circular No. 7: Soil Nail Walls
**Scope:** Design guidance for soil nail walls including global stability, facing design, pullout resistance, and corrosion protection. Required FS ≥ 1.35 (static), ≥ 1.1 (seismic).

## AASHTO LRFD Bridge Design Specifications — Section 11
**Scope:** Slope stability requirements for embankments and cut slopes supporting bridges. Resistance factors for slope stability analysis.

## Duncan & Wright (2005) — Soil Strength and Slope Stability
**Scope:** Comprehensive reference for slope stability theory and practice. Strength selection, pore pressure modeling, three-dimensional effects, reliability analysis, and back-analysis methods.

## USBR Design Standards No. 13, Chapter 4 — Static Stability Analyses
**Scope:** Slope stability analysis for embankment dams. Loading cases, shear strength selection, pore pressure modeling, minimum FS requirements (1.3 steady-state, 1.5 end-of-construction for some agencies).

## Spencer (1967) — A Method of Analysis of the Stability of Embankments Assuming Parallel Inter-Slice Forces
**Scope:** Rigorous method satisfying both force and moment equilibrium. Parallel inter-slice force inclination. Generally preferred over Bishop for non-circular failure surfaces.

## Morgenstern & Price (1965) — The Analysis of the Stability of General Slip Surfaces
**Scope:** Most general limit equilibrium method. Variable inter-slice force function. Satisfies all equilibrium conditions. Standard in modern software (SLOPE/W, SLIDE).
