# Standards Reference — Hydropower Fundamentals

## FERC Hydropower Licensing
**Scope:** US Federal Energy Regulatory Commission regulates non-federal hydropower. Licensing process (Original License, New License, Exemption), environmental review (NEPA), fish passage requirements, minimum flows, recreation, and dam safety. All non-federal hydro projects on navigable waters or federal lands require FERC license.

## IEC 60193 — Hydraulic Turbines, Storage Pumps and Pump-Turbines — Model Acceptance Tests
**Scope:** International standard for hydraulic turbine model testing. Test procedures, measurement accuracy, cavitation testing, and performance acceptance criteria. Results from model tests are used to predict prototype performance via similarity laws.

## IEC 60041 — Field Acceptance Tests to Determine the Hydraulic Performance of Hydraulic Turbines, Storage Pumps and Pump-Turbines
**Scope:** International standard for field performance testing of installed hydraulic machines. Measurement methods (flow, head, power, efficiency), test procedures, and acceptance criteria. Complementary to IEC 60193 (model tests).

## IEC 61116 — Electromechanical Equipment Guide for Small Hydroelectric Installations
**Scope:** Guidance for small hydro (< ~5 MW). Turbine selection, generator specification, control systems, and protection. Simplified approach suitable for small/micro hydro projects where full IEC 60193 model testing is not economically justified.

## ASCE MOP 79 — Steel Penstocks (2nd ed.)
**Scope:** Design of steel penstocks for hydropower. Shell thickness, stiffener design, anchor blocks, expansion joints, surge analysis, bends and wye branches, and fabrication. The primary US reference for penstock structural design.

## IEEE 1020 — Guide for Control of Small (Unattended) Hydroelectric Plants
**Scope:** IEEE guide for automation and control of small hydroelectric plants. SCADA systems, protection relays, automatic start/stop, synchronization, and remote monitoring. Covers both grid-connected and islanded operation.

## USBR Design of Small Dams (2007), Chapter 18 — Powerplant Planning
**Scope:** USBR guidance for preliminary design of powerplants at dams. Site investigation, head and flow analysis, turbine selection, powerhouse layout, and economic analysis. Practical design-level guidance with worked examples.

## ESHA (2004) — Guide on How to Develop a Small Hydropower Plant
**Scope:** European Small Hydropower Association comprehensive guide. Hydrological analysis, turbine selection, civil works, electrical equipment, economics, environmental assessment, and permitting. Widely used internationally for small hydro project development.
