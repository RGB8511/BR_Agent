# Standards Reference — Field Load Testing

## ASTM D1143 — Standard Test Methods for Deep Foundations Under Static Axial Compressive Load
**Scope:** Procedures for static compression load testing of single piles and drilled shafts. Slow Maintained Load, Quick Maintained Load, Constant Rate of Penetration, and cyclic loading procedures. Reaction system requirements, instrumentation (dial gauges, LVDT, tell-tales, strain gauges), data recording, and reporting. Annex covers bidirectional (O-cell) testing.

## ASTM D3689 — Standard Test Methods for Deep Foundations Under Static Axial Tensile Load
**Scope:** Static tension (uplift) load testing procedures. Loading increments, hold times, measurement requirements, and failure criteria for uplift capacity verification. Reaction frame and instrumentation requirements.

## ASTM D3966 — Standard Test Methods for Deep Foundations Under Lateral Load
**Scope:** Static lateral load testing of piles and drilled shafts. Single-pile and pile-group procedures. Measurement of lateral deflection, rotation, and load. Inclinometer measurements for deflected shape. Results used to validate p-y curve analysis.

## ASTM D4945 — Standard Test Method for High-Strain Dynamic Testing of Deep Foundations
**Scope:** PDA (Pile Driving Analyzer) testing procedure. Transducer installation, data acquisition, CASE method capacity estimation, signal matching (CAPWAP), and reporting. Covers initial driving, restrike, and static load test correlation.

## ASTM D7383 — Standard Test Methods for Axial Compressive Force Pulse (Rapid) Testing of Deep Foundations
**Scope:** Statnamic and similar rapid load test methods. Instrumentation, data recording, unloading point method analysis, rate effect correction, and comparison with static tests. Test setup, safety, and reporting requirements.

## ASTM D1196 — Standard Test Method for Nonrepetitive Static Plate Load Tests of Soils and Flexible Pavement Components
**Scope:** Surface plate load test for modulus of subgrade reaction and bearing capacity. Plate sizes, loading increment procedures, settlement measurement, and k-value determination. Used for pavement design and shallow foundation verification.

## ASTM D4394 — Standard Test Method for Determining the In Situ Modulus of Deformation of Rock Mass Using Rigid Plate Loading Method
**Scope:** Rigid plate loading on prepared rock surface (in pit, adit, or tunnel). Deformation modulus determination for rock mass design. Loading/unloading cycles, settlement measurement, and modulus calculation.

## PTI DC35.1 — Recommendations for Prestressed Rock and Soil Anchors
**Scope:** Post-Tensioning Institute standard for ground anchor design and testing. Performance test, proof test, extended creep test, and lift-off test procedures. Acceptance criteria (elastic elongation, creep rate), lock-off procedures, and long-term monitoring. The primary standard for anchor testing in the US.

## FHWA-SA-99-015 — Ground Anchors and Anchored Systems (GEC-4)
**Scope:** FHWA geotechnical engineering circular covering ground anchor design, construction, and testing. Comprehensive testing procedures complementing PTI. Includes design charts, construction considerations, and QA/QC requirements.

## ASTM D5882 — Standard Test Method for Low Strain Impact Integrity Testing of Deep Foundations
**Scope:** Pile Integrity Test (PIT) procedure. Hammer impact, accelerometer measurement, signal interpretation for length verification and impedance change detection. Limitations and appropriate use for quality assurance of drilled shafts and driven piles.

## ASTM D6760 — Standard Test Method for Integrity Testing of Concrete Deep Foundations by Ultrasonic Crosshole Testing
**Scope:** Cross-hole Sonic Logging (CSL) procedure. Access tube requirements, equipment calibration, testing procedure, data interpretation (first arrival time, signal energy), and defect characterization criteria.
