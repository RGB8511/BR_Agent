# Standards Reference — Concrete Mix Design

## ACI 211.1 — Standard Practice for Selecting Proportions for Normal, Heavyweight, and Mass Concrete
**Scope:** Step-by-step procedure for proportioning concrete mixes using absolute volume method. Covers normal weight (2155–2560 kg/m³), heavyweight (for radiation shielding), and mass concrete. Tables for water content, w/cm vs. strength, coarse aggregate volume fractions.

## ACI 211.2 — Standard Practice for Selecting Proportions for Structural Lightweight Concrete
**Scope:** Mix design for lightweight concrete using expanded shale, clay, or slate aggregates. Special considerations for aggregate absorption (pre-wetting), unit weight control, and pumping.

## ACI 318 — Building Code Requirements for Structural Concrete
**Scope:** Chapter 19 specifies durability requirements: maximum w/cm, minimum f'c, air content, and cement type by exposure class (F, S, W, C categories). Table 19.3.2 is the primary durability specification table.

## ASTM C192 — Standard Practice for Making and Curing Concrete Test Specimens in the Laboratory
**Scope:** Procedures for mixing, molding, and curing laboratory specimens. Specifies mixer types, mixing sequence (coarse agg + some water → cement + FA + remaining water), consolidation (rodding or vibration), and curing (lime-saturated water at 23 ± 2°C).

## ASTM C143 — Standard Test Method for Slump of Hydraulic-Cement Concrete
**Scope:** Slump cone test. 300mm cone, filled in 3 layers, 25 roddings per layer. Measure subsidence. Not suitable for very stiff (< 15mm) or very fluid (> 230mm) mixes — use slump flow (C1611) for SCC.

## ASTM C231 — Standard Test Method for Air Content of Freshly Mixed Concrete (Pressure Method)
**Scope:** Type A and Type B pressure meters. Most common field test for air content. Not valid for lightweight or highly porous aggregates (use volumetric method C173 instead).

## ASTM C138 — Standard Test Method for Density (Unit Weight), Yield, and Air Content (Gravimetric)
**Scope:** Weigh a known volume of fresh concrete. Calculate unit weight, yield (volume produced per batch), and gravimetric air content. Essential for verifying mix proportions.

## ASTM C494 — Standard Specification for Chemical Admixtures for Concrete
**Scope:** Types A through G chemical admixtures. Specifies performance requirements: water reduction, setting time change, and compressive strength relative to control at 1, 3, 7, 28, and 365 days.

## ASTM C260 — Standard Specification for Air-Entraining Admixtures for Concrete
**Scope:** Requirements for air-entraining agents. Must produce stable air void system with adequate spacing factor (verified by ASTM C457 on hardened concrete).
