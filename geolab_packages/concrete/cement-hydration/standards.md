# Standards Reference — Cement Chemistry

## ASTM C150 — Standard Specification for Portland Cement
**Scope:** Chemical and physical requirements for Types I–V portland cement. Specifies limits on compound composition, fineness, setting time, strength, heat of hydration, and soundness.

## ASTM C595 — Standard Specification for Blended Hydraulic Cements
**Scope:** Cements blended at the plant: Type IL (portland-limestone), IS (portland-slag), IP (portland-pozzolan), IT (ternary). Increasingly replacing separate cement + SCM batching.

## ASTM C618 — Standard Specification for Coal Fly Ash and Raw or Calcined Natural Pozzolan
**Scope:** Requirements for fly ash (Class F, C) and natural pozzolans (Class N). Chemical requirements (SiO₂+Al₂O₃+Fe₂O₃ ≥ 50% for Class C, ≥ 70% for Class F), loss on ignition (≤ 6%), fineness (≤ 34% retained on #325), strength activity index (≥ 75% of control at 7 or 28 days).

## ASTM C989 — Standard Specification for Slag Cement for Use in Concrete and Mortars
**Scope:** GGBFS graded by strength activity index: Grade 80 (low), Grade 100 (moderate), Grade 120 (high activity). Higher grades develop strength faster.

## ASTM C1240 — Standard Specification for Silica Fume Used in Cementitious Mixtures
**Scope:** Requirements for silica fume: SiO₂ ≥ 85%, moisture ≤ 3%, LOI ≤ 6%. Strength activity index ≥ 105% at 7 days.
