# Standards Reference — Soil Sampling

## ASTM D1586 — Test Method for Standard Penetration Test (SPT) and Split-Barrel Sampling of Soils
**Scope:** SPT procedure and split-spoon sampling. Equipment (sampler, hammer, drill rod), procedure (30 cm drive increments, blow counts), recording requirements, and sample handling. Note: This standard covers both the SPT (see SPT package) and the disturbed sample obtained.

## ASTM D1587 — Practice for Thin-Walled Tube Sampling of Fine-Grained Soils for Geotechnical Purposes
**Scope:** Thin-wall (Shelby) tube sampling. Tube dimensions (OD, wall thickness, cutting edge angle, area ratio), push procedure (smooth, continuous, no hammering), sample retention, extraction, sealing, labeling, and handling.

## ASTM D3550 — Practice for Thick Wall, Ring-Lined, Split Barrel, Drive Sampling of Soils
**Scope:** Thick-walled split-barrel sampling with removable ring liners. California sampler (2.5 in and 3 in). Produces partially disturbed samples in stiff soils. Common in western US practice for residual and alluvial soils.

## ASTM D4220 — Practice for Preserving and Transporting Soil Samples
**Scope:** Procedures for preserving, packaging, and transporting soil samples to the laboratory. Four sample groups (A through D) with increasing preservation requirements. Covers containers, sealing, labeling, temperature control, vibration protection, and storage.

## ASTM D6519 — Practice for Sampling of Soil Using the Hydraulically Operated Stationary Piston Sampler
**Scope:** Fixed-piston (Osterberg-type) sampler procedure. Equipment requirements, piston locking mechanism, push procedure, sample retention, extraction, and handling. The standard for highest-quality tube sampling of soft to medium clays.

## Hvorslev (1949) — Subsurface Exploration and Sampling of Soils for Civil Engineering Purposes
**Scope:** The foundational reference for soil sampling theory. Area ratio, inside clearance ratio, cutting edge design, piston samplers, sample disturbance mechanisms, and sampler design principles. Still relevant and widely cited despite its age.

## Ladd & DeGroot (2003) — Recommended Practice for Soft Ground Site Characterization (Arthur Casagrande Lecture)
**Scope:** State-of-practice for investigating soft clay sites. Sampling methods, sample quality assessment, laboratory testing program design, SHANSEP methodology for strength, and integrated site characterization approach. Essential reference for soft clay projects.
