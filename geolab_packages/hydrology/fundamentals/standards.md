# Standards Reference — Hydrology Fundamentals

## USDA-NRCS TR-55 — Urban Hydrology for Small Watersheds (1986)
**Scope:** Methods for estimating runoff volume (CN method), peak discharge (graphical peak discharge method), hydrographs (tabular method), and storage routing for small watersheds (up to ~2500 acres). Defines SCS rainfall distributions (Type I, IA, II, III). Primary reference for curve number tables and the dimensionless unit hydrograph.

## USDA-NRCS NEH Part 630 — Hydrology (National Engineering Handbook)
**Scope:** Comprehensive NRCS hydrology reference. 22 chapters covering precipitation, runoff, unit hydrographs, hydrologic soil groups, curve numbers, storm distributions, flood routing, and water balance. Supersedes many aspects of TR-55 with updated methods.

## NOAA Atlas 14 — Precipitation-Frequency Atlas of the United States
**Scope:** Point precipitation frequency estimates for all US states and territories. Durations: 5 minutes to 60 days. Return periods: 1 to 1000 years. Available online (NOAA PFDS). The standard source for IDF curve data in the US. Supersedes earlier TP-40, HYDRO-35, and NWS publications.

## HEC-HMS — Hydrologic Modeling System (USACE)
**Scope:** USACE software for rainfall-runoff simulation. Implements loss models (SCS CN, Green-Ampt, initial/constant), transform methods (SCS UH, Clark, Snyder, ModClark), baseflow, routing (Muskingum, modified Puls), and reservoir operations. Standard of practice for watershed modeling.

## FAO Irrigation and Drainage Paper No. 56 — Crop Evapotranspiration
**Scope:** Defines the Penman-Monteith reference ET equation (ET₀) and crop coefficient (Kc) method for estimating crop water requirements. The international standard for ET estimation.

## ASCE Manual of Practice No. 70 — Evapotranspiration and Irrigation Water Requirements
**Scope:** ASCE guidance on ET estimation methods, lysimeter measurements, and irrigation scheduling. Complements FAO-56 with US-specific guidance.

## ASTM D5242 — Standard Practice for Operating Rain Gauges
**Scope:** Procedures for installation and operation of tipping-bucket and weighing precipitation gauges. Siting requirements, maintenance, and data quality.
