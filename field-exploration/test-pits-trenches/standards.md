# Standards Reference — Test Pits, Trenches & Shallow Exploration

## OSHA 29 CFR 1926 Subpart P — Excavations
**Scope:** Federal safety standard for all excavation work. Soil classification (Types A/B/C), protective systems (sloping, benching, shoring, shielding), competent person requirements, inspection frequency, spoil placement, means of egress, and atmospheric testing. Mandatory compliance for all excavation deeper than 1.5 m (5 ft).

## ASTM D1452 — Standard Practice for Soil Exploration and Sampling by Auger Borings
**Scope:** Procedures for advancing auger borings (hand and power) for soil investigation. Covers continuous-flight, bucket, and hand augers. Sample collection, logging, and documentation requirements.

## ASTM D2488 — Standard Practice for Description and Identification of Soils (Visual-Manual)
**Scope:** Field procedures for describing and classifying soils using the Unified Soil Classification System (USCS) without laboratory testing. Color, grain size estimation, plasticity estimation (thread/ribbon test), moisture, consistency, structure, and cementation. The fundamental standard for all field soil logging.

## ASTM D4220 — Standard Practices for Preserving and Transporting Soil Samples
**Scope:** Sample handling, preservation, and transportation to prevent disturbance and moisture change. Group A (moisture only), Group B (classification), Group C (undisturbed — engineering properties), Group D (highly sensitive, fragile). Packaging, labeling, and chain-of-custody requirements.

## USACE EM 1110-1-1804 — Geotechnical Investigations
**Scope:** USACE manual covering all aspects of geotechnical investigation including test pits, trenches, borings, and in-situ testing. Investigation planning, field procedures, sampling, logging, and reporting. Chapter on test pits covers equipment, logging, safety, and sampling.

## FHWA GEC-5 — Geotechnical Site Characterization (NHI-16-072)
**Scope:** FHWA guidance on comprehensive geotechnical site characterization. Chapter on subsurface exploration methods includes test pits, borings, in-situ testing, and geophysics. Covers exploration planning, method selection, and integration of results.

## ISRM Suggested Methods — Rock Discontinuity Description
**Scope:** International Society for Rock Mechanics methods for describing rock discontinuities in exposures. Orientation, spacing, persistence, roughness (JRC), wall strength, aperture, infilling, seepage, and number of sets. Essential for mapping rock faces exposed in test pits and trenches.

## ASTM D420 — Standard Guide for Site Characterization for Engineering Design and Construction Purposes
**Scope:** Comprehensive guide for planning and conducting geotechnical investigations. Covers phased investigation approach (desk study, preliminary, detailed), exploration method selection, sampling, testing, and reporting. Framework standard that references other ASTM methods.
