# Standards Reference — Bearing Capacity

## Terzaghi (1943) — Theoretical Soil Mechanics
**Scope:** Original bearing capacity theory for strip footings on homogeneous soil. Nc, Nq, Nγ factors. Foundation of all subsequent bearing capacity methods.

## Meyerhof (1963) — Some Recent Research on Bearing Capacity of Foundations
**Scope:** Extended Terzaghi's solution with shape, depth, inclination, and ground surface inclination factors. Updated Nc, Nq, Nγ factors.

## Hansen (1970) — A Revised and Extended Formula for Bearing Capacity
**Scope:** Further generalization with base tilt, ground slope, and improved shape/depth/inclination factors. Widely used in international practice.

## Vesic (1973) — Analysis of Ultimate Loads of Shallow Foundations
**Scope:** Modified Nγ factor and inclination factors. Comprehensive treatment of eccentric and inclined loading. Commonly used in US practice alongside Meyerhof.

## AASHTO LRFD Bridge Design Specifications
**Scope:** Section 10 — Foundations. Bearing capacity design using resistance factors. Strength limit state: φ = 0.45 (rational method, no load test) to 0.55 (with field verification). Service limit state settlement checks.

## FHWA GEC-006 — Shallow Foundations
**Scope:** FHWA guidance for highway bridge shallow foundations. Step-by-step bearing capacity and settlement calculations. Worked examples for spread footings on sand and clay.

## ASTM D1194 — Standard Test Method for Bearing Capacity of Soil for Static Load and Spread Footings (Withdrawn)
**Scope:** Plate load test procedure. Now replaced by references in ASTM D1143 and local practice. Provides field verification of bearing capacity.

## IS:6403 — Code of Practice for Determination of Bearing Capacity of Shallow Foundations
**Scope:** Indian standard incorporating Meyerhof and Hansen factors. Widely used internationally alongside AASHTO/FHWA methods.
