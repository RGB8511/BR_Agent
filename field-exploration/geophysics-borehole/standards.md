# Standards Reference — Borehole Geophysics & Logging

## ASTM D7400 — Standard Test Methods for Downhole Seismic Testing
**Scope:** Procedures for downhole seismic surveys to determine Vs and Vp profiles. Covers equipment (source, geophones, recording), field procedures (clamping, source triggering, reversal for S-wave identification), data reduction (first-arrival picking, interval velocity calculation), and reporting. The primary standard for downhole Vs measurement.

## ASTM D4428 — Standard Test Methods for Crosshole Seismic Testing
**Scope:** Procedures for crosshole seismic surveys between two or more boreholes. Covers borehole preparation (PVC casing, grout, deviation survey), equipment, source/receiver coupling, data acquisition, and velocity calculation. Requires accurate deviation survey of all boreholes (critical for velocity accuracy).

## ASTM D5753 — Standard Guide for Planning and Conducting Borehole Geophysical Logging
**Scope:** General guidance for planning borehole geophysical logging programs. Log selection based on objectives, borehole preparation, quality assurance, depth control, calibration, and reporting. Covers all standard log types.

## USACE EM 1110-1-1802 — Geophysical Exploration for Engineering and Environmental Investigations
**Scope:** Chapters on borehole geophysics cover all standard log types: natural gamma, SP, resistivity, sonic, caliper, neutron, density, televiewer, temperature, and flowmeter. Includes interpretation guidelines, quality control, and case studies for USACE projects.

## Keys (1990) — Borehole Geophysics Applied to Ground-Water Investigations (USGS TWI)
**Scope:** USGS Techniques of Water-Resources Investigations manual. Comprehensive guide to borehole geophysical logging for hydrogeological applications. Theory, field procedures, interpretation, and case studies. Covers nuclear, electrical, acoustic, and caliper logs. Freely available from USGS.

## ISRM Suggested Methods — Borehole Geophysics in Rock Engineering
**Scope:** International Society for Rock Mechanics guidelines for borehole geophysics in rock mass characterization. Covers televiewer interpretation, fracture logging from borehole images, seismic methods in rock, and integration with core logging and surface mapping data.

## ASTM D6167 — Standard Guide for Conducting Borehole Geophysical Logging — Mechanical Caliper
**Scope:** Field procedures for mechanical caliper logging. Three-arm and four-arm configurations, calibration, logging speed, depth control, and interpretation for borehole diameter and shape assessment.
