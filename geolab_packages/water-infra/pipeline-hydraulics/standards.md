# Standards Reference — Pipeline Hydraulics

## AWWA M11 — Steel Pipe: A Guide for Design and Installation (5th ed.)
**Scope:** Comprehensive AWWA manual for steel water pipe. Hydraulic design (Hazen-Williams, Darcy-Weisbach), wall thickness design, external loads, thrust restraint, fittings, lining and coating, and installation. The primary reference for steel water pipeline design in North America.

## AWWA M23 — PVC Pipe: Design and Installation (3rd ed.)
**Scope:** AWWA manual for PVC water pipe. Hydraulic properties (C = 150), pressure classes, external loading, bedding requirements, thrust restraint, and installation. Covers C900 (transmission) and C905 (large diameter) PVC pipe.

## ASCE Manual of Practice No. 79 — Steel Penstocks
**Scope:** Design of steel penstocks for hydropower. Hydraulic design, shell thickness (Barlow), stiffener design, anchor blocks, expansion joints, surge analysis, and fabrication. Applicable to high-head water conveyance generally.

## AWWA C200 Series — Steel Water Pipe Standards
**Scope:** Manufacturing and design standards for steel water pipe. C200 (fabrication), C205 (cement-mortar lining), C208 (dimensions for fittings), C210 (coal-tar coatings), C213 (fusion-bonded epoxy lining/coating), C222 (polyurethane lining/coating).

## Wylie & Streeter (1993) — Fluid Transients in Systems
**Scope:** The definitive reference for hydraulic transient (water hammer) analysis. Method of Characteristics, column separation, surge tanks, pump transients, valve operations, and transient control devices. Basis for most commercial transient analysis software.

## AWWA M51 — Air Valves: Air-Release, Air/Vacuum, and Combination
**Scope:** Selection, sizing, and placement of air valves in water pipelines. Air release valve sizing for operating conditions, air/vacuum valve sizing for filling and draining, and combination valve selection. Placement criteria based on pipeline profile.

## Crane Technical Paper 410 — Flow of Fluids Through Valves, Fittings, and Pipe
**Scope:** Industry-standard reference for minor loss coefficients (K values) and equivalent lengths for valves and fittings. Covers all common fitting types and valve styles. Widely used across all piping industries.
